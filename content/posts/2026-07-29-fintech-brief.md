# Fintech Brief — July 29, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### RBI Launches 'Finquiry' — Structured Fintech Engagement on July 31

The Reserve Bank of India has invited fintech companies to a structured interaction event called **'Finquiry'**, scheduled for July 31, 2026, from 3 PM to 5 PM in Mumbai. Registration closes today at 6 PM.

The event is expected to cover **tokenization, regulatory compliance frameworks**, and broader fintech-banking integration topics. This signals RBI's continued push toward proactive engagement with the fintech ecosystem rather than purely top-down regulation.

For fintechs operating in payments, lending, and digital assets, this is a meaningful signal — the central bank wants dialogue, not just directives. Registration is through the RBI's official channels. [Source: RBI on X](https://x.com/RBI/status/2081706319682355386)

### India's GDP Growth Forecast Slashed to 6.6% on Oil Shock and Weak Investment

A Reuters poll of 42 economists (conducted July 21–27) projects India's GDP growth at **6.6% in FY27**, down sharply from 7.7% in FY26, with only a modest recovery to 6.8% in FY28. The twin headwinds: **weak private investment** and **elevated oil prices** tied to the Iran conflict.

The rupee, however, caught a break — RBI likely intervened in the forex market on July 28, with dollar sales and slumping oil prices helping the currency build on a rally. The central bank is also expected to **hold rates through 2026**, prioritizing growth risks over inflation. ### Krutrim's Third Round of Layoffs — 200+ Departures and Counting

Ola's AI venture Krutrim has conducted its **third round of layoffs**, cutting nearly 50 employees from the linguistics team. Total departures now exceed **200 people** across multiple rounds since June, including senior leadership exits.

The company has pivoted from its ambitious full-stack AI roadmap (including the Bodhi 1 chip) toward **AI cloud services**, reporting 3x revenue growth and its first net profit in FY26. But the continued workforce reduction — particularly from the linguistics team that once had 600+ people training Indic-language models — raises questions about whether India's homegrown AI race can sustain its talent intensity. [Source: YourStory](https://yourstory.com/ai-story/ola-krutrim-sees-three-senior-leadership-exits-layoffs-in-lingusitics-team-report) · [Source: Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/topic/krutrim-layoffs)

### Inflexor Ventures Hits First Close of ₹1,250 Cr Fund III

Early-stage VC firm **Inflexor Ventures** has announced the first close of its **₹1,250 crore ($131M) Fund III**, securing ₹400 crore (~$42M) in initial commitments. The fund is anchored by the Self-Reliant India (SRI) Fund, **HDFC AMC**, and HDFC AMC Select AIF FoF, with international institutional participation.

Fund III will target **pre-Series A and Series A** investments with cheque sizes of ₹15–45 crore, aiming for a portfolio of 22–25 companies. Inflexor manages ~₹1,300 crore in AUM with 27 portfolio companies including Atomberg, Kale Logistics, and Entropik.
