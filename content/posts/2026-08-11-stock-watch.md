## 📰 Sector News

- **UPI MDR framework evolves** — Lok Sabha passed the **Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026**, removing the legal bar on MDR charges for digital payments. **PCI clarified: UPI will remain free for consumers and small merchants.** Any merchant service charge would be a commercial arrangement between merchants and payment providers, not a charge on customers. NPCI-led UPI and Services Steering Committee to examine MDR specifics post Parliament clearance. Finance Minister Sitharaman: "No decision has been made."[^1][^2]
