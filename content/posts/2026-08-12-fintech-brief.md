# Fintech Brief — August 12, 2026

## RBI Governor: AI Could Do for Lending What UPI Did for Payments

RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra delivered a landmark address at the [FIBAC 2026](https://www.fortuneindia.com/amp/story/business-news/ai-could-reshape-banking-like-liberalisation-digitalisation-did-rbi-governor-sanjay-malhotra/153206) conference in Mumbai, declaring that AI could reshape banking the way liberalisation did in the 1990s and digitalisation did in the 2010s. He urged banks to move beyond pilot projects and adopt AI as a core strategic capability.

Key takeaways:

- **Credit inclusion**: AI models using alternative data — cash flows, GST filings, utility payments — can expand the "frontier of bankability" by assessing borrowers without traditional credit histories.
- **Digital Payment Intelligence Platform**: The RBI will build a new platform to help banks deploy AI/ML for detecting increasingly sophisticated digital payment fraud ([Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/digital/rbi-to-equip-banks-with-new-platform-to-counter-rising-digital-payment-fraud-107362.htm)).
- **Human oversight is non-negotiable**: Malhotra warned that banks cannot transfer responsibility for decisions to AI systems or vendors. Boards must approve AI governance frameworks, and banks should conduct red-team exercises and stress tests on models.
- **UPI as foundation**: India's DPI stack — UPI, Aadhaar, Account Aggregators, ULI — gives banks a unique structural advantage to build AI-driven financial services.

The Governor also revealed that [BRICS nations are discussing linking fast payment systems with digital currencies](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/banking/brics-cross-border-payments-cbdc-upi-local-currencies-rbi-malhotra-126081100860_1.html), signalling continued momentum for cross-border UPI expansion.

## UPI Charges: Government Clarifies Consumers Won't Pay

After the [Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2026/Aug/10/cant-face-facts-finance-minister-nirmala-sitharaman-hits-out-at-cpim-mp-brittas-communists) was passed in Parliament last week, Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman explicitly clarified that [UPI transactions will remain free for consumers](https://indiantelevision.com/itv-news/sitharaman-says-upi-transactions-will-remain-free-for-consumers). Any future Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) would apply only to large merchants on transactions above ₹2,000, leaving an estimated 96% of transactions unaffected ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/economy/story/heres-how-much-mdr-could-be-charged-on-upi-and-rupay-transactions-548523-2026-08-11)).

However, economist [Ajit Ranade argued that introducing MDR could undermine UPI's growth trajectory](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/economy/story/keep-upi-free-for-everybody-economist-says-mdr-wont-affect-consumers-is-incorrect-logic-548493-2026-08-11), pointing out that zero MDR was a key driver of UPI's adoption — it made digital payments resemble cash. The debate over whether a "nominal" merchant levy will create a two-tier payment ecosystem is far from settled.

## Accel Closes Oversubscribed $550M India Fund

Global VC firm [Accel has raised $550 million for its ninth India-focused fund](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/11/accel-closes-oversubscribed-550m-india-fund-within-weeks-19-months-after-its-last/), just 19 months after closing its $650 million Fund VIII. The fund was oversubscribed and closed within weeks, taking Accel's total India capital to $1.2 billion across both funds ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/startup/accel-raises-550-million-for-ninth-india-fund-doubles-down-on-ai-14002259.html)).

Sector focus remains AI (treated as a horizontal layer across all sectors), fintech, consumer internet, software, and — notably — advanced manufacturing, which is emerging as a new pillar. Accel's partners indicated they are seeing a quality inflection in Indian founders, with AI applications and enterprise software being the most active deal flow categories. The fund is expected to begin deployment in 2027.

India's VC fundraising has crossed $3.2 billion in H1 2026 alone, with nearly 10 India-focused funds announced or closed.

## RBI Defers Loan Recovery Agent Norms to January 2027

The RBI has [deferred its guidelines on loan recovery agents and their engagement](https://www.livemint.com/companies/news/accel-returns-to-india-market-with-550-million-fund-bets-big-on-ai/amp-11786426098370.html) from October 2026 to January 2027, giving lenders an additional three months to comply ([Mint](https://www.facebook.com/mint.live/posts/mintprimer-the-reserve-bank-of-india-rbi-on-6-august-said-its-norms-on-loan-reco/1512996097536772)). The norms are designed to prevent excessive coercion by recovery agents — a long-standing consumer abuse issue in Indian lending.

While the extension gives banks more time, it also means borrowers continue facing aggressive recovery practices for longer. The guidelines, when implemented, will mandate specific conduct standards, recording of recovery interactions, and accountability frameworks for both banks and their third-party agents.
