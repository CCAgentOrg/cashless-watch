## 📰 Sector News

- **Apple Pay coming to India in October — UPI support may be delayed** — Apple has held advanced discussions with HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank to provision cards. **UPI integration expected at a later stage** — pending discussions with NPCI over technical differences on biometric authentication (Apple wants device-level biometric auth; NPCI wants UIDAI-backed authentication). Apple's NFC architecture is another sticking point as India's payments ecosystem is built around interoperability between apps. Launch expected October 2026.[^5]
- **RBI Governor urges banks to accelerate AI adoption** — At FIBAC 2026 (Mumbai), RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra outlined **5 ways AI can transform banking**: (1) Credit underwriting using alternative data (GST filings, utility bills), (2) Voice interfaces in Indian languages for financial inclusion, (3) Real-time fraud detection, (4) AI to beat AI-delivered frauds, (5) AI as a board-driven strategic priority. "AI can extend the frontier of bankable India considerably," he said.[^6]
- **Rajya Sabha passes Payment & Settlement Systems Amendment Bill** — Parliament cleared the bill enabling potential MDR on UPI. **No immediate consumer charges** — NPCI-led committee to examine specifics. Cost of running UPI ecosystem estimated at ₹20,000 Cr/year. Government aims for financial sustainability while keeping consumer/small-merchant transactions free.[^7]
- **AvenuesAI (formerly Infibeam) Q1 FY27 results** — Revenue doubled to ₹2,680 Cr (+109% YoY), PAT +45% to ₹84.8 Cr. But sequential decline of 5% in PAT and margin compression (3.7% vs 5.5%) dragged shares -5.87%. Company guiding FY27 revenue at ₹11,000–13,000 Cr.
