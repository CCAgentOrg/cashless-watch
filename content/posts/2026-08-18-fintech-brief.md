# Fintech Brief — August 18, 2026

## RBI Proposes Harmonised Interest Rate Framework: Quarterly Resets, Borrower Consent Mandatory

The Reserve Bank of India has released draft directions that would require banks and NBFCs to reset floating-rate loan benchmarks at least once every three months — a significant compression from the current cycle where borrowers can wait years for rate adjustments to reflect policy changes.

The proposed *RBI (Interest Rates on Loans and Advances) Directions, 2026*, slated to take effect from April 1, 2027, would also mandate borrower consent before any benchmark switch and prohibit changes that disadvantage the borrower. A one-time migration of existing loans to the new framework by April 1, 2029 is proposed, with explicit safeguards: no rate increase at the point of migration and no migration charges.

This is the most consequential consumer-facing lending reform since the 2016 external benchmark mandate. By forcing quarterly resets, the RBI is effectively ensuring that repo rate movements transmit to EMIs within 90 days — closing the notorious lag where banks were quick to pass on rate hikes but sluggish with cuts. Comments are open until September 11, 2026 via the [Connect 2 Regulate](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMonetaryPolicyParameters.aspx) platform.

Separately, the RBI also released draft directions on a [revised leverage ratio framework](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/DraftNotificationsGuildelines.aspx) and a [revised Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA) framework](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/DraftNotificationsGuildelines.aspx) for commercial banks, both proposed from April 2027.

## UPI Closes FY26 at 24,161 Crore Transactions, 49% of Global Real-Time Payments

NPCI's full-year figures for FY 2025-26 confirm what monthly data had been signalling: UPI processed a record 24,161.69 crore transactions worth ₹314.23 lakh crore. Volume has grown more than fivefold from FY22's 4,595.61 crore transactions.

July 2026 alone saw 23.66 billion transactions worth ₹29.88 lakh crore — a 4.1% month-on-month increase. UPI now accounts for approximately 49% of all real-time payment transactions globally and roughly 85% of India's total digital payment volume by transaction count. Over 700 banks are now connected to the network.

The data, cited in a [parliamentary reply](https://www.pib.gov.in/) and widely reported this week, underscores UPI's entrenchment as infrastructure rather than product. The declining average ticket size suggests deeper adoption for micro-transactions, not just high-value use cases.

## GFF 2026: Agentic AI Emerges as the Defining Theme for Indian Banking

At the ongoing [Global Fintech Fest 2026](https://www.economictimes.indiatimes.com/default-subsec/spotlight/gff-2026-why-agentic-ai-is-becoming-a-key-theme-for-financial-services/articleshow/133294941.cms) in Mumbai, agentic AI has emerged as the dominant technology theme — moving beyond generative AI assistants to systems that can autonomously execute multi-step workflows.

SBI and HDFC Bank are reportedly deploying agentic AI for fraud detection, compliance screening, and loan processing pipelines. The distinction matters: where traditional automation follows predefined rules and generative AI assists humans with answers, agentic AI assesses situations and executes sequences of actions toward defined outcomes — processing a loan application from onboarding through underwriting to decision, for instance.

The festival's focus signals where Indian financial institutions are placing their next big bets. For consumers, the near-term implication is faster loan processing and potentially more responsive customer service. The longer-term question — around accountability, bias, and regulatory oversight of autonomous financial decision-making — remains open.
