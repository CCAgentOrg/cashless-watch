# Fintech Brief — August 21, 2026

## UPI Transaction Fees Expected Within Two Weeks

The government is preparing to announce a **0.3% Merchant Discount Rate (MDR)** on UPI transactions of ₹2,000 and above, with an official notification expected within two weeks. The move follows the passage of legislation earlier this month that laid the legal groundwork for ending India's zero-MDR regime for merchant payments — while keeping peer-to-peer transactions free.

Industry experts have pushed back, arguing that zero MDR for small merchants and low-value payments must be retained to protect UPI's financial inclusion gains. UPI processed a record **23.66 billion transactions** worth **₹29.88 lakh crore** in July 2026, according to NPCI data, making it the backbone of India's digital payments ecosystem. The challenge now: designing a tiered MDR structure that recovers infrastructure costs without pricing small merchants out of digital payments.

## Navi Raises $100 Million from Prosus Ahead of Planned IPO

Sachin Bansal-founded fintech **Navi** has secured **$100 million** from Dutch technology investor **Prosus** at a valuation of **$1.3 billion** — marking the company's first institutional capital raise. The investment is subject to regulatory approvals, including clearance from the Competition Commission of India.

The round comes as Navi prepares for a potential IPO targeting **$2 billion** in valuation and up to **$315 million** in proceeds. Navi has been building a full-stack financial services play: its UPI app was India's fourth-largest by volume in July (holding ~4% market share), its lending arm Navi Finserv crossed **₹13,000 crore** in assets under management in FY26, and it reported consolidated profitability in Q4 FY26. Prosus, which already backs PayU in India, is placing a pre-IPO bet on a profitable fintech platform — a notable shift from the growth-at-all-costs era.

## Upstox Explores $350–400 Million IPO

Tiger Global-backed investment platform **Upstox** has initiated preliminary discussions with investment bankers for a **$350–400 million IPO**, planning a mix of Offer for Sale and fresh issue. The company has crossed **2 crore registered customers**, positioning itself as one of India's largest retail brokerage platforms.

Upstox's IPO plans add to the pipeline of Indian fintech public listings, alongside Navi. The combination of a mature customer base and Tiger Global's backing could make it one of the more closely watched tech IPOs of the coming quarters.

## DPIIT Partners with PhonePe and Shell India for Startup Ecosystem

The Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) has partnered with **PhonePe** and **Shell India** to improve startup access to payment infrastructure, mentorship, and investor networks. The collaboration aims to help innovative businesses scale sustainably while supporting India's technology-led economic growth.

For PhonePe, the partnership deepens its positioning beyond a payments app into an ecosystem infrastructure player — leveraging its UPI rails and merchant network to support the broader startup community.
