# Fintech Brief — August 25, 2026

## UPI Completes a Decade: From 1.78 Crore to 24,162 Crore Annual Transactions

The Unified Payments Interface, launched by NPCI on August 25, 2016, turns ten today — and the numbers are staggering. Annual transaction volume surged nearly 13,000-fold, from 1.78 crore in FY 2016-17 to 24,162 crore in FY 2025-26. Transaction value hit ₹314 lakh crore in the same period, while monthly volumes touched a record 2,366 crore in July 2026.

A PIB backgrounder released ahead of the anniversary highlights that 741 banks are now live on the UPI network. Peer-to-merchant (P2M) transactions are heavily skewed toward small-ticket payments — 86% are below ₹500 — underscoring UPI's deep integration into everyday retail. The platform now operates across 11 countries, with Greece and the Maldives as the latest adoptees. UPI accounts for nearly 49% of global real-time payment transactions by volume.

The finance ministry signalled that the next decade will focus on expanding merchant acceptance and deepening financial inclusion through continued policy support and technological advancement.

## Navi Scores $100M from Prosus, First Institutional Backing Ahead of Reported IPO

Sachin Bansal's Navi has secured $100 million from Prosus — the first time the Bengaluru-based payments and lending platform has accepted institutional capital. The investment comes amid reports that Navi is preparing to list on Indian stock exchanges.

Navi reported consolidated revenues of $323.3 million (₹2,691 crore) for the year ending March 2026, though net losses widened from $13.2 million to approximately $48.6 million. Bansal described the Prosus investment as "a strong endorsement of the institution we're building at Navi." Prosus's parent Naspers has a long India track record, having first invested in Flipkart during its 2012 Series D.

## Indian Startup Funding Picks Up: $198M Across 21 Deals This Week

Indian startups raised $198.33 million across 21 deals in the week ending August 22 — a 31% week-on-week increase, with fintech leading the funding wave. The broader funding environment continues to show recovery momentum after the extended winter.

Separately, Third Wave Coffee raised ₹408 crore in a round led by WestBridge Capital, and healthtech startup Arintra (Austin HQ, Bengaluru engineering) closed a $25 million Series B led by Define Ventures for its autonomous medical coding platform.
