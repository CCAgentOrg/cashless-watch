# Fintech Brief — September 10, 2026

## UPI Goes Shopping in Singapore: FOMO Pay Deal Puts UPI QR at Resorts World Sentosa

NPCI International and Singapore-headquartered payments institution FOMO Pay announced a strategic partnership at Global Fintech Fest on September 9 to enable UPI QR code acceptance for merchants across Singapore, with Resorts World Sentosa — home to Universal Studios Singapore — among the first to go live: Indian visitors can already book and pay online for experiences using their preferred UPI apps ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/npci-international-partners-fomo-pay-to-expand-upi-acceptance-across-singapore-article-14026018.html), [Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/nri/visit/indian-travellers-can-now-use-upi-to-pay-at-singapores-resorts-world-sentosa/articleshow/133961210.cms)). Acceptance will expand progressively across FOMO Pay's merchant network. The consumer math is straightforward: Singapore received 1.2 million Indian visitors in 2025 ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/npci-international-partners-fomo-pay-to-expand-upi-acceptance-across-singapore-article-14026018.html)), and paying over UPI rails lets travellers see the amount in familiar terms instead of fumbling with card forex markups at checkout. The fine print still matters — the conversion rate and any embedded charges on cross-border UPI transactions are set by the banks and partners in the chain, so check your app's receipt when the facility spreads to more merchants. It's day-3 proof of the direction Modi set on Tuesday: UPI acceptance following Indian travellers abroad, merchant by merchant.

## NPCI and NVIDIA Open an AI Training Gym for Banking Agents

NPCI has announced an open reinforcement-learning environment for banking AI agents, developed in collaboration with NVIDIA, giving institutions what it calls a stable, reusable and cost-effective platform to develop and evaluate banking AI agents ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/npci-nvidia-unveil-open-ai-training-environment-for-banking-agents-using-synthetic-data-554347-2026-09-09)). The key consumer-protective detail: the environment runs on synthetic data, so banks can train and stress-test their AI agents without exposing real customer records in the process. That matters because banks are clearly headed toward agentic AI — systems that don't just answer questions but execute actions on accounts. A shared, open testing ground is a better starting point than each bank experimenting on live customers. But a training gym is not a rulebook: it says nothing about what happens when a bank's AI agent makes a costly mistake with your money, or who bears the liability. That's the gap Reuters reporting flagged earlier this week on NPCI's Unified Agent Protocol for agentic UPI payments — a liability framework is reportedly planned, with details undisclosed. Before agentic banking scales, consumers should get the same clarity merchants and banks get.

## RBI Drains a Record ₹11 Trillion Surplus as the Rupee Breaks 95 — and Widens Retail Forex Access

The Reserve Bank of India has begun draining a record banking-system liquidity surplus — around ₹11 trillion, roughly $115 billion — using short-term sell/buy dollar-rupee forex swaps, some maturing in October, according to people familiar with the operations ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-09/inr-usd-india-rbi-uses-currency-swaps-to-reduce-115-billion-cash-surplus), [PTI/Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/news/economy/finance/india-rbi-uses-currency-swaps-to-cut-115-billion-cash-surplus/articleshow/133969593.cms)). The surplus built up after massive inflows under RBI's recent capital-raising plans; in a sell/buy swap the near leg immediately pulls rupees out of the banking system. Why consumers should care: "sell/buy swaps can push the liquidity problem into the future, but not eliminate it," as Emkay Global economist Madhavi Arora put it ([Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/news/economy/finance/india-rbi-uses-currency-swaps-to-cut-115-billion-cash-surplus/articleshow/133969593.cms)) — cheap money sloshing through the system is an inflation risk, and inflation is a regressive tax. Context for the timing: the rupee fell 34 paise to close at 95.08 as Brent crude crossed $100 ([New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/business/2026/Sep/09/rupee-on-a-losing-streak-falls-under-95-as-oil-crosses-100-rbi-taps-dlr-swaps-to-suck-out-liquidity)). On the constructive side, RBI announced at GFF that its FX-Retail platform will add five currency pairs — euro, pound, Swiss franc, Canadian dollar and UAE dirham — with yen, Aussie, Singapore dollar and more planned, all to be available through Bharat Connect ([Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/news/economy/finance/rbi-to-expand-retail-forex-access-with-five-new-currency-pairs-plans-wider-global-currency-bouquet/articleshow/133971792.cms)). More retail forex access is genuinely good for consumers: transparent, exchange-based rates beat airport-counter spreads.

## Deals: QNu Labs Raises ₹200 Crore, Uber Doubles Down on Carrum, RentoMojo's OFS-Heavy IPO Opens

Quantum-safe cybersecurity startup **QNu Labs** raised ₹200 crore in a Series A1 led by the National Quantum Mission and Speciale Invest, with Sony Innovation Fund, Gaja Capital and Artha Ventures participating — taking its total raised to ₹375 crore ([ET Telecom](https://telecom.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/industry/qnu-labs-raises-200-crore-in-series-a1-from-nqm-speciale-invest-others-to-scale-rd-expand-markets/133959299)). Notable: a government mission anchoring a private startup round is still rare in India. **Carrum Mobility**, a B2B fleet-management startup, raised $10 million (~₹96 crore) from Uber — its second Uber bet this year after $7 million in January — at a post-money valuation of around ₹1,600 crore ([EquityPandit](https://www.equitypandit.com/carrum-mobility-raises-10-million-from-uber-to-expand-business)). Furniture rental platform **RentoMojo** opened its IPO on September 9 at a price band of ₹384-404, targeting ₹1,256 crore at a ~₹4,206 crore valuation — but read the structure carefully: only ₹150 crore (12%) is fresh money into the company, while ₹1,106 crore is an offer for sale by existing shareholders, and ₹70 crore of the fresh portion goes to repaying borrowings ([Indian Startup News](https://indianstartupnews.com/news/rentomojo-sets-ipo-price-band-at-rs-384-404-issue-opens-september-9-12490785)). Also in motion: at-home salon startup **Yes Madam** is in advanced talks to raise ₹250 crore from Xponentia Capital Partners, Riverwalk Holdings and existing investor Info Edge Ventures ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/startup/yes-madam-in-talks-to-raise-rs-250-crore-from-xponentia-riverwalk-as-at-home-services-heat-up-14025650.html/amp)).
