# Fintech Brief — September 14, 2026

## BRICS Adopts New Delhi Declaration: Payments Plumbing, Not a Currency

The 18th BRICS Summit closed in New Delhi on Saturday with the [New Delhi Declaration adopted unanimously](https://m.economictimes.com/news/economy/finance/brics-leaders-push-faster-cheaper-cross-border-payments-deeper-use-of-local-currencies/articleshow/134131084.cms), and for payments watchers the language is a study in deliberate modesty. The declaration encourages the BRICS Payment Task Force to continue work on cross-border payment mechanisms — building on the Kazan and Rio initiatives — studying interoperability of payment and messaging channels and promoting trade settlement in members' own currencies, while explicitly recording that there will be "no one-size-fits-all approach" ([Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/news/economy/finance/brics-leaders-push-faster-cheaper-cross-border-payments-deeper-use-of-local-currencies/articleshow/134131084.cms)). No common currency, no roadmap, no date — as expected, India's chairship steered the bloc away from the headline-grabbing "BRICS currency" toward narrower plumbing: [interoperability, local-currency settlement, and an RBI-proposed link-up of members' central bank digital currencies](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/india-push-brics-digital-currency-link-despite-hurdles-sources-say-2026-09-10) — digital rupee to digital yuan to digital real — rather than any shared unit ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/india-push-brics-digital-currency-link-despite-hurdles-sources-say-2026-09-10)). The summit also added a financial-integrity layer: BRICS nations committed to disrupt money laundering and fraud in cross-border payment systems, and for the first time agreed to explore [an extradition mechanism and asset-recovery steps for economic offenders](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/grouping-eyes-mechanism-for-extradition-of-eco-fugitives/articleshow/134157738.cms) ([Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/grouping-eyes-mechanism-for-extradition-of-eco-fugitives/articleshow/134157738.cms)). The consumer stake: cheaper cross-border rails eventually show up in remittance costs and import prices — but temper expectations. The Rio declaration of 2025 said much the same, analysts see limited progress given India-China frost, and the RBI's own governor has framed BRICS CBDC talk as about lowering payment costs, not de-dollarisation ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/india-push-brics-digital-currency-link-despite-hurdles-sources-say-2026-09-10)). Watch what the Payment Task Force ships before the next summit, not what declarations say.

## Udaan Acquires Swiggy's LYNK for $53 Million — All Paper, No Cash

B2B commerce platform **Udaan** has agreed to acquire **LYNK**, Swiggy's retail distribution business, in a share-swap deal valuing LYNK at ₹500 crore (~$53 million) — settled by issuing preference shares in Trustroot Internet, Udaan's parent, giving Swiggy a minority stake instead of cash ([VCCircle](https://www.vccircle.com/b2bplatform-udaan-to-bag-swiggy-s-retail-distribution-biz-in-share-swap-deal)). LYNK generated about ₹668 crore in revenue in FY26, with Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai and Kolkata contributing roughly 75% of it ([BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/41343b50-07dc-43e1-87e4-363957ff973e)); the deal follows Udaan's $160 million recapitalisation, and the company claims ~25% revenue CAGR over the ten quarters to Q1 CY2026 with EBITDA burn down about 70% ([VCCircle](https://www.vccircle.com/b2bplatform-udaan-to-bag-swiggy-s-retail-distribution-biz-in-share-swap-deal)). Swiggy, meanwhile, pivots from running B2B distribution directly to holding indirect exposure through its Udaan stake. The consumer stake: B2B distribution is the invisible layer that decides what kirana shelves stock and at what price — consolidation concentrates pricing power, and a share-swap means Swiggy is betting on Udaan's recovery rather than cashing out. Deal was announced September 7 ([Tech in Asia](https://www.techinasia.com/news/indias-udaan-acquire-swiggyowned-lynk-53m)).

## Weekend Wrap

- **Zeta targets global profitability in FY27.** The banking-tech infrastructure firm told Mint it expects its global business to post a full year of profitability in FY27, after several quarters of operational profitability abroad and three-plus profitable years in India — the payoff of long sales and implementation cycles finally reaching scale ([Mint via TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/news/moodys:5626c09b5f7a3:0-zeta-targets-global-profitability-in-fy27-as-overseas-business-scales)).
- **Tokenised real estate enters the chat.** Maharashtra IT minister Ashish Shelar launched "The Box" by RealX and MST Blockchain, with Liminal Custody, in Mumbai — pitching fractional, blockchain-based real-estate ownership as the next frontier after payments ([FinTech BizNews](https://www.fintechbiznews.com/fintech-technology/nex-frontier-digi-titles)). A useful contrast with last week's Demat 2.0 bond pilot: that one works because SEBI and RBI built the regulatory guardrails first. Tokenised property with no such scaffolding is a pitch, not a product — ask who holds the title before who holds the token.
