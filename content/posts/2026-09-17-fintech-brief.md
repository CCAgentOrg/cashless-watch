# Fintech Brief — September 17, 2026

## The UPI MDR Meets Its First Legal Challenge

One day after NPCI's rate card landed, the framework is in court. Advocate Anjan Datta has [filed a public interest litigation in the Supreme Court seeking to quash the September 14-15 gazette notifications behind the 0.4% MDR on UPI merchant payments above ₹2,000](https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/plea-in-supreme-court-chances-centres-decision-to-introduce-charges-on-upi-transactions-above-2-000-550329), naming the Union government, RBI, NPCI and the UPI & Services Steering Committee as respondents ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/banking/pil-in-sc-challenges-0-4-mdr-on-upi-transactions-above-2-000-in-india-126091601409_1.html)). The petition challenges the amended payment law itself, the [differing treatment of UPI and RuPay debit-card transactions](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/banking/pil-in-sc-challenges-0-4-mdr-on-upi-transactions-above-2-000-in-india-126091601409_1.html), and — the ground that should concern every payment app — argues the charges will ultimately reach the common citizen despite assurances otherwise. The framework it targets: 0.4% on P2M transactions above ₹2,000 capped at ₹300, a flat ₹5 for railways, telecom, insurance, fuel and agricultural inputs, and 0.02% capped at ₹300 for capital-market transactions, per the [petition's own recital of the rates](https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/plea-in-supreme-court-chances-centres-decision-to-introduce-charges-on-upi-transactions-above-2-000-550329). Meanwhile the fine print kept arriving: NPCI's FAQ confirms [small merchants in the P2PM category receiving up to ₹1 lakh a month via UPI QR stay at zero MDR even on a single payment above ₹2,000](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/upi-mdr-merchant-fees-small-businesses-transactions-charges-payments-126091600417_1.html). Markets read the opportunity, not the litigation: Paytm touched a 52-week high on Tuesday before [paring gains Wednesday, while Mobikwik slipped into the red](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/stocks/paytm-one-mobikwik-shares-jump-up-to-5-after-upi-mdr-announcement-brokerages-see-revenue-boost-14030798.html); JM Financial estimates ₹210 crore of incremental FY27 revenue for Paytm and raised its target price to ₹2,150, while flagging that [how the MDR pool is split across the value chain remains undecided](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/stocks/paytm-one-mobikwik-shares-jump-up-to-5-after-upi-mdr-announcement-brokerages-see-revenue-boost-14030798.html) — which is precisely where the consumer-protection question lives. Politics has followed: the BJP defended the charge, [NCP(SP) attacked it](https://telanganatoday.com/bjp-defends-upi-mdr-ncpsp-criticises-charge-on-transactions-above-rs-2000), and [NITI Aayog's Vice-Chairman argued businesses must be self-sustainable](https://m.economictimes.com/news/economy/policy/niti-aayog-vc-defends-upi-merchant-fee-says-businesses-must-be-self-sustainable/amp_articleshow/134290063.cms). Watch the pass-through, not the petition: whether a ₹25,000 purchase quietly carries ₹100 of MDR inside the price is the test of the "consumers pay nothing" promise.

## Supreme Court to RBI: Enforce the Rules on Vehicle Repossession

In a ruling that should read like a compliance memo to every NBFC collections desk, the Supreme Court held on Wednesday that [a financier's self-help right to repossess a hypothecated vehicle cannot be exercised through force](https://www.livelaw.in/supreme-court/financiers-cannot-repossess-vehicles-by-force-supreme-court-awards-rs-10-lakh-compensation-to-truck-owner-550357), and [directed the RBI to enforce its own repossession guidelines on NBFCs and scheduled commercial banks](https://money.rediff.com/news/market/sc-to-rbi-enforce-lawful-vehicle-seizure-by-lenders/54445020260916) so that borrowers are not dispossessed without due process. The case ended with the court [awarding ₹10 lakh in compensation to a truck owner whose vehicle was seized without following the rulebook, with Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company on the wrong side of the order](https://www.whalesbook.com/news/English/lawcourt/Supreme-Court-Orders-RBI-to-Enforce-Vehicle-Repossession-Rules-CIFCL-Fined/6aab1ef1ed7f8e81734e2d24). RBI's guidelines already require lawful means, agent due diligence and no harassment — the court's point is that the central bank must ensure genuine compliance, not merely publish circulars. For consumers financing vehicles, the practical takeaway is documentation: a repossession done by stealth or force is now compensable, and the regulator is on notice to act.

## Peak XV and Sequoia Exit ₹1,999 Crore of Groww

The post-IPO unlock continues at India's largest retail broker: [10.41 crore shares of Billionbrains Garage Ventures — 1.69% of Groww's equity — changed hands at ₹192 apiece through block deals worth roughly ₹1,999 crore](https://tradebrains.in/indian-markets/groww-stock-falls-4-after-peak-xv-and-sequoia-offload-169-stake-worth-1999-cr-12539583), with [Peak XV Partners and Sequoia Capital's growth fund the sellers reported a day earlier](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/groww-shares-fall-2-5-after-1-69-equity-sold-in-rs-1-999-crore-block-deal-peak-xv-sequoia-likely-sellers-14030784.html/amp). The stock fell as much as 4.65% to ₹188.40 against a floor price of ₹191.45, and the sellers face a 30-day lock-in on further sales ([Upstox](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/stocks/groww-shares-decline-4-65-amid-block-deal-buzz-here-is-what-is-known-so-far/article-200327)). Pre-deal, Peak XV held 15.68% and the Sequoia fund 1.46%. This is the third big early-backer exit in weeks — [Y Combinator sold nearly 1.2% for ₹1,435 crore in August](https://upstox.com/news/market-news/stocks/groww-shares-decline-4-65-amid-block-deal-buzz-here-is-what-is-known-so-far/article-200327), following Ribbit Capital — even as the company [reported Q1 FY27 consolidated profit of ₹735 crore, up 94.3% year-on-year](https://tradebrains.in/indian-markets/groww-stock-falls-4-after-peak-xv-and-sequoia-offload-169-stake-worth-1999-cr-12539583). Venture funds harvesting into strength is normal; the question for retail shareholders is whether steady promoter-backed supply at every rally caps the multiple.

## PB Fintech Tightens Its House

PolicyBazaar parent PB Fintech's M&A committee met on September 16 and [approved buying the remaining 20% of MyLoancare Ventures for up to ₹5 crore, making the housing-finance marketplace a wholly-owned subsidiary after a September 30 valuation cutoff](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/pb-fintech-approves-5-crore-acquisition-remaining-stake-myloancare/51126266). The board also authorised [₹10 crore of capital into digital car-care platform PB Wheels and an additional ₹1 crore into PB Financial, its account aggregator, to meet RBI net-worth norms](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/pb-fintech-approves-5-crore-acquisition-remaining-stake-myloancare/51126266). The transaction needs only post-facto RBI intimation since control already exists. Small numbers, but the direction is legible: with its [payment-aggregator licence freshly in hand](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/companies/pb-fintech-approves-5-crore-acquisition-remaining-stake-myloancare/51126266), PB Fintech is consolidating lending distribution, payments and data pipes under one roof — a full-stack financial supermarket where every subsidiary is another consumer touchpoint worth watching.
