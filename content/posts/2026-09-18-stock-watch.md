## 📰 Sector News

- **UPI MDR is now law of the land:** Gazette notification (Sep 14) + NPCI FAQs (Sep 15) confirm **0.4% MDR on P2M UPI transactions above ₹2,000, effective Oct 15, 2026**, capped at ₹300 for tickets ≥₹75,000. P2P stays free, ≤₹2,000 stays free, and small merchants receiving ≤₹1 lakh/month via UPI QR are exempt. UPI apps are barred from charging platform fees. A **PIL in the Supreme Court** challenges the fee; industry split — PhonePe's Sameer Nigam and Paytm's Vijay Shekhar back it, Zerodha's Nithin Kamath and Ashneer Grover oppose.
- **NSE IPO is live:** anchor book raised **₹6,746 crore**, led by LIC's ₹450 crore anchor commitment (LIC holds 10.72% pre-IPO). Exchange stocks (BSE +0.59% Thursday, CDSL +3.88%) traded on the attention.
- **HDFC Bank succession:** two CEO candidates formally submitted to the RBI — internal contender Kaizad Bharucha plus an external candidate. The stock is 2026's weakest Nifty Bank name (−29% YTD); clarity is the bull case.
- **RBI/NPCI rollout mode from GFF 2026:** UPI Tap & Pay (PIN-less up to ₹5,000), MyUPI (AI support via NPCI's FiMI model + a Safety Switch to freeze debits), Unified Ticketing Platform pilot, RuPay Tap on Phone, passkey biometrics, and UPI International cross-border merchant payments. The **agentic-payments protocol (UAP) is on hold** pending NPCI safeguards.
- **Macro drains:** RBI absorbed ₹3.93 lakh crore via VRRR and is selling ₹1 lakh crore of gilts via OMOs (first tranche Sep 17); rupee closed 95.94 after breaching 96; Fed's hike + hawkish dots keep FII flows fragile.
- **Digital gold under the scanner:** RBI/SEBI oversight with physical-backing requirements proposed — a compliance cost line for wallet/fintech apps offering gold.
