# Fintech Brief — September 28, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### 1. Bank strike called off at the eleventh hour: branches open today, but October 26 still looms

The three-day nationwide bank strike scheduled for September 28–30 was [deferred late Sunday night after a 9:30 PM meeting between the Indian Banks' Association and the United Forum of Bank Unions](https://indianexpress.com/article/business/banking-and-finance/all-india-bank-strike-deferred-10896610). Per the UFBU's September 27 circular, [the IBA agreed to immediately constitute a high-level committee on the unions' core demand — declaring all remaining Saturdays holidays for a five-day banking week — which will examine alternatives, consult stakeholders and "work towards a solution while keeping customers' interests in mind"](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/banking/finance/banking/banks-open-on-september-28-nationwide-strike-deferred-after-union-agreement-sbi-hdfc-pnb-axis-bank-kotak-mahindra-icici-bank-of-india/articleshow/134529410.cms). Note the word: deferred, not cancelled. [The unions' indefinite strike call from October 26 remains on the table if the demand stays unresolved](https://m.economictimes.com/news/new-updates/bank-strike-today-full-list-of-banks-facing-work-disruption-will-hdfc-bank-axis-bank-sbi-pnb-icici-bank-remain-closed/articleshow/134521043.cms). The timing was never casual — the strike would have run into September 30, the half-yearly closing that drives reconciliation, provisioning and treasury operations, which is why the government had already [advanced central government salaries, wages and September pensions to September 25](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/3-day-bank-strike-from-september-28-centre-government-employees-to-get-salary-in-advance-on-september-25-banks-to-open-this-sunday-advisory-issued-to-customers/articleshow/134454930.cms) as a precaution. For customers: normal branch services today, and a committee report to judge before the next strike deadline.

### 2. UPI MDR reaches the Supreme Court: first PIL heard today, 17 days before the fee begins

The 0.4% merchant charge on high-value UPI gets its first day in court today — [a PIL by advocate Anjan Datta is listed before a bench led by Chief Justice of India Surya Kant, with the Centre, RBI, NPCI and the UPI & Services Steering Committee as respondents](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/cji-led-bench-to-hear-plea-against-mdr-on-upi-payments-today/amp). The petition challenges the Finance Ministry's September 14 notification and the September 15 MDR framework (effective October 15), arguing the levy arrived "without adequate statutory safeguards, transparency or public consultation" — no disclosed cost studies, no methodology, and thresholds that create cliffs (₹2,001 attracts a percentage fee; ₹2,000 doesn't) while the ₹300 cap favours very high-value transactions. Datta's sharpest point: [identical "no-charge" protection continues for RuPay debit cards without any monetary ceiling](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/cji-led-bench-to-hear-plea-against-mdr-on-upi-payments-today/amp), and "a bare direction against an expressly recognised economic consequence does not eliminate the burden" — the pass-through ban notwithstanding. The street pressure keeps building alongside: [the All India Mobile Retailers Association has now announced a national "No UPI Day" on October 2](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/upi-must-remain-zero-mdr-mobile-retailers-announce-october-2-protest/articleshow/134517944.cms), and [the government is examining fuel dealers' demand for an MDR exemption](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/govt-likely-to-examine-demand-for-upi-mdr-exemption-for-petrol-pumps-14037301.html) after Punjab and Madhya Pradesh pumps threatened to refuse UPI above ₹2,000 from mid-October — even though the framework gives fuel a flat ₹5 rate. NPCI's counter-narrative, from CEO Dilip Asbe's Thursday remarks: [96% of UPI volume and 75% of value sit outside the MDR net, most chargeable value comes from merchants who already accept credit cards, and the levy could recover ₹13,000–15,000 crore in year one](https://www.financialexpress.com/business/news/75-of-merchants-insulated-from-upi-mdr-npci-ceo/4346563). The court will now test whether the numbers' legal foundation holds.

### 3. SEBI's settlement overhaul: siphoned money "should come back first"

SEBI's board-approved [Settlement of Administrative and Civil Proceedings Regulations, 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/markets-news-sebi-settlement-overhaul-may-aid-recovery-of-diverted-funds-126092700204_1.html) could change how investors actually get money back in fund-diversion cases. The new framework formula-links settlement amounts, treats wrongful gains separately — disgorgement plus Remedial and Regulatory Terms stack on top of the settlement cheque — and introduces a US Wells-style settlement notice *before* the show-cause notice, giving entities a 60-day window to settle early. Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey put the philosophy plainly: ["if there is a siphoning of money, it should come back first. That is non-negotiable"](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/markets-news-sebi-settlement-overhaul-may-aid-recovery-of-diverted-funds-126092700204_1.html) — whole-time member Kamlesh Chandra Varshney added the point is getting money back "with interest" while proceedings would otherwise drag on in court while "one doesn't know the situation of the company." There's also a one-time 90-day window (at a 20% premium) for parties whose earlier settlement applications were rejected, withdrawn or never filed under the 2018 regulations. The catch, which SEBI itself hasn't settled: whether listed companies must disclose receiving a pre-SCN settlement notice —Varshney's answer was "most probably" not, but "we will examine that particular aspect." For consumers, faster recovery is good news; an undisclosed settlement channel that skips material-event disclosure is the part to watch. The norms take effect 30 days after notification.

### 4. Moneyview's ₹1,092-crore IPO closes today on a 6x note; deeptech capital keeps flowing

Digital-lending fintech Moneyview closes its [₹1,091.68-crore IPO today, having been subscribed 6.01 times after day two — bids for over 139 crore shares against 23.25 crore on offer](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/ipo/moneyview-ipo-continues-to-see-robust-subscription-on-day-2-booked-nearly-3-times-so-far-check-gmp-14038123.html), with grey-market premium running 41–46% ahead of the [October 1 listing](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/ipos/fpos/moneyview-ipo-enters-day-2-subscription-reaches-1-4x-gmp-signals-38-potential-listing-gain-should-you-bid/articleshow/134473917.cms). The issue — ₹750 crore fresh plus an offer for sale by promoters Puneet Agarwal and Sanjay Aggarwal and investors [Accel, Tiger Global and Ribbit Capital](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/ipo/fintech-firm-moneyview-seeks-624-million-valuation-in-trimmed-india-ipo-126092100110_1.html) — carries FY26 financials showing revenue up 43.3% to ₹3,351.2 crore but profit up just 1.2% at ₹244.1 crore; [₹325 crore of the fresh proceeds go straight into loan disbursals under default-loss-guarantee arrangements](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/ipo/moneyview-ipo-gmp-today-september-24-check-subscription-status-of-moneyview-should-you-apply-or-avoid-14037161.html), a structure where the fintech shares credit risk. That growth-over-margin profile is exactly what public investors are being asked to price in a post-MDR, tighter-commission world. Meanwhile early-stage capital pointed elsewhere: [the IITM Unicorn Frontier Fund-I — from IIT Madras, its Research Park and Unicorn India Ventures — closed its first ₹450 crore, already deploying ₹55 crore across four deeptech startups](https://m.economictimes.com/tech/newsletters/morning-dispatch/a16zs-raghu-raghuram-on-indias-ai-potential-insurance-cos-navigate-new-proposal/articleshow/134529607.cms).
