# Fintech Brief — September 29, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### 1. Supreme Court won't stop the UPI fee — but the government must now defend it on affidavit

The UPI MDR survives its first court test: [a bench of CJI Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohana on Monday refused an interim stay on the 0.4% merchant charge for UPI payments above ₹2,000, but issued notices to the Centre, RBI and NPCI returnable within four weeks](https://www.rediff.com/news/report/sc-refuses-to-stay-mdr-on-upi-seeks-govts-response/20260928.htm). The October 15 rollout stands. [The petition by advocate Anjan Datta challenges the September 14 gazette notification under Section 10A of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act and the September 15 MDR framework](https://www.thestatesman.com/india/sc-refuses-to-stay-mdr-on-upi-transactions-above-rs-2000-seeks-centre-rbi-npci-response-1503644054.html) — and [LiveLaw reports the court wants the government to explain, on affidavit, the basis for ending nearly six years of free UPI](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/supreme-court-asks-centre-to-explain-decision-to-levy-charges-on-upi-transactions-refuses-stay-552055). The government's core defence: [MDR affects only ~4% of merchant transactions, is neither a tax nor a government levy, and the money stays within the payments ecosystem](https://entrackr.com/news/sc-declines-stay-on-upi-mdr-rollout-seeks-response-from-centre-rbi-npci-on-plea-12585634) — while [P2P transfers (37% of volume, 70% of value) and small merchants stay free](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/india/supreme-court-issues-notice-to-rbi-npci-over-upi-mdr-charges-above-rs2-000-14039899.html). The fine print still bites: [a flat ₹5 for railways, telecom, insurance, fuel and agri inputs; 0.02% capped at ₹300 for mutual funds, securities and brokers](https://entrackr.com/news/sc-declines-stay-on-upi-mdr-rollout-seeks-response-from-centre-rbi-npci-on-plea-12585634). What the affidavit will have to reckon with: [UPI carried 2,451 crore transactions worth ₹29.82 lakh crore in August 2026 alone](https://theprobe.in/governance/upi-payments-levy-supreme-court-centre-affidavit-12585393) — scale built on being free — and [street resistance is organising, with mobile retailers' "No UPI Day" set for October 2](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/sc-refuses-interim-stay-on-mdr-for-upi-transactions-asks-centre-to-explain-decision/articleshow/134535885.cms). Four weeks is the window in which the government's cost study, if one exists, must surface.

### 2. Moneyview's IPO closes at ~73x — institutions swamped the book on the last day

The ₹1,091.68-crore Moneyview IPO closed Monday at [72.96x overall, with the QIB book exploding from 0.25x after day two to 163.36x in the final official snapshot at 5:15 PM — after briefly touching 227x mid-afternoon](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/ipo/moneyview-ipo-day-3-subscribed-11-44x-retail-nii-demand-surge/52120453). Small HNIs booked [108.48x](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/ipo/moneyview-ipo-day-3-subscribed-11-44x-retail-nii-demand-surge/52120453); retail closed at [14.01x](https://scanx.trade/stock-market-news/ipo/moneyview-ipo-day-3-subscribed-11-44x-retail-nii-demand-surge/52120453). Grey-market premium climbed to [~41%, implying a listing near ₹48 against the ₹34 upper band](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/ipos/fpos/moneyview-ipo-day-3-gmp-signals-41-listing-premium-issue-subscription-reaches-6x-should-you-apply/articleshow/134531121.cms) when shares debut October 1. The issue — [₹750 crore fresh plus ₹341.68 crore offer-for-sale by promoters and Accel, Tiger Global and Ribbit Capital, at a trimmed ~$624 million valuation](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/ipo/fintech-firm-moneyview-seeks-624-million-valuation-in-trimmed-india-ipo-126092100110_1.html) — asks public investors to price a growth-over-margin digital lender (FY26 revenue up 43% to ₹3,351 crore; profit up just 1.2%) in the same fortnight its own merchant borrowers start paying MDR. Allotment finalises today.

### 3. HDFC Bank's next chief: RBI's call expected within days, and the market is pricing the transition

[The RBI is expected to pick HDFC Bank's next MD & CEO within 7–10 days, ahead of incumbent Sashidhar Jagdishan's term ending October 26 — with ICICI Prudential Life's Anup Bagchi the board's first preference and Deputy MD Kaizad Bharucha the internal alternative](https://www.moneycontrol.com/banking/bagchi-or-bharucha-rbi-to-take-a-call-on-hdfc-bank-s-new-ceo-in-7-10-days-article-14036883.html). The regulator is doing unusual diligence: [seeking feedback from IRDAI and ICICI Bank on Bagchi's candidature given his three-year gap from mainstream banking](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/banking/finance/sc-refuses-interim-stay-on-0-4-mdr-for-upi-merchant-payments-above-rs-2000-seeks-centre-rbi-npci-response-to-plea/amp_articleshow/134535281.cms). [Jefferies argues the stock's rerating "hinges on CEO transition and execution" — HDFC Bank is down ~27% this year, among the worst Nifty 50 performers](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/hdfc-bank-s-rerating-hinges-on-ceo-transition-and-execution-jefferies-126092800492_1.html). The regulator dislikes interim arrangements, so expect the name before the bank's October 17 results, where the incoming CEO is expected to appear alongside Jagdishan.

### 4. The lending SRO fills its board with lenders: PhonePe and Navi chiefs join FACE

[FACE, the RBI-recognised fintech self-regulatory organisation, on Monday elected Hemant Gala (CEO, PhonePe Lending) and Rajiv Naresh (MD & CEO, Navi) to its board as member directors](https://www.tradingview.com/news/moodys:427921a473dd6:0-face-elects-hemant-gala-rajiv-naresh-as-member-directors) — joining KreditBee's Madhusudan Ekambaram, Uni Cards' Nitin Gupta, Moneyview's Sanjay Aggarwal and Vivriti Capital's Vineet Sukumar. FACE writes the digital-lending conduct standards its members follow, so board composition is consumer policy by another name: every member director now sits atop a lending business. An SRO is only as tough as its least conflicted member — worth tracking how FACE's next conduct code treats fee disclosure and recovery practices.
