# Fintech Brief — September 30, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### 1. Apple Pay lands in India — cards only, no UPI, and the fee fight decides who joins next

After nearly a decade of waiting, Apple Pay went live in India on Wednesday with [eligible Axis Bank credit cards on the Visa and Mastercard networks as the only supported instruments](https://livefromalounge.com/breaking-apple-pay-arrives-in-india-enabled-with-axis-bank-credit-cards-as-of-september-30-2026). The rollout is real but phased: [users are reporting they can now add Axis cards to Apple Wallet via the Axis Bank app and verify with Face ID, though confirmed in-store tap payments are still trickling in](https://www.smartprix.com/bytes/apple-pay-finally-launches-in-india-with-axis-bank-support). What India's default payment habit won't get: [Apple Pay does not touch UPI, which handles roughly 84% of the country's digital payment volume](https://www.theapplepost.com/2026/09/29/72820/apple-pay-reportedly-launching-in-india-with-axis-bank-today) — this is a tokenised NFC credit-card play in a market where scan-the-QR-and-pay-from-bank-account is the norm. The launch cast is telling: [HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and SBI Card are absent at launch, with negotiations ongoing over Apple's reported ask of about 20 basis points per transaction — a significant bite out of the roughly 40–50 bps margin in the payments layer](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/apple-pay-set-to-launch-in-india-with-axis-bank-today-sources-say). [RuPay and American Express don't work either, and debit cards aren't supported yet](https://www.cnbctv18.com/india/apple-pay-launches-in-india-axis-bank-credit-cards-visa-mastercard-upi-rupay-support-20001538.htm). Why now: [regulatory changes permitting biometric authentication for card payments finally accommodated Apple's Face ID/Touch ID model](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/articles/foldables-ai-servers-india-expansion-092447891.html), and [Apple Pay VP Jennifer Bailey says "the pieces came together"](https://www.hindustantimes.com/business/the-pieces-came-together-jennifer-bailey-on-why-apple-pay-lands-in-india-now-101790733997418.html). Consumer take: genuinely useful for travellers and premium-card users — but with [NPCI having rolled out its own UPI Tap & Pay this month](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/how-apple-pay-could-stack-up-among-indias-payment-apps/134449113), tap-to-pay is no longer Apple's differentiator here. The interesting fight isn't at the terminal; it's over that 20 bps, which will decide how many Indian banks ever show up.

### 2. SBM Bank India hunts for a fintech co-owner

[SBM Bank India — the first foreign bank to receive a universal banking licence under the RBI's wholly-owned-subsidiary model — is reviving plans to induct a fintech investor, working with EY as sell-side advisor to sell up to a 26% stake at a target valuation of around ₹1,200 crore, Moneycontrol reports](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/sbm-bank-india-revives-plans-to-induct-fintech-partner-taps-ey-for-minority-stake-sale-14040845.html). The pitch, per people quoted: tie up with "a suitable fintech player," leverage synergies, and bolster the balance sheet — with a financial investor as fallback and RBI's final nod required for any deal. [A November 2025 RBI notification opened the door, allowing foreign-bank subsidiaries in India to dilute to 74% or less and list on local exchanges](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/sbm-bank-india-revives-plans-to-induct-fintech-partner-taps-ey-for-minority-stake-sale-14040845.html). The consumer angle: SBM has been a niche but real piece of banking plumbing for cross-border and embedded-finance flows, and a fintech becoming a 26% owner of its own partner bank is exactly the kind of related-party tangle that deserves scrutiny before it closes, not after.

### 3. RBI's liquidity ledger: a decade-record ₹1 trillion out the door, bigger state cash lines proposed

Two RBI items on the plumbing beat. First, [the central bank has net sold ₹1 trillion of government bonds this financial year — its biggest annual net sale in over a decade — as it drains surplus liquidity, and treasury officials expect the total to double by December](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/india-central-bank-completes-1-trillion-rupee-net-debt-sale-first-time-decade-2026-09-28/). Second, [an RBI-appointed panel led by former Karnataka additional chief secretary ISN Prasad has proposed raising the states' Ways and Means Advances corpus by 11.2% to ₹67,839 crore, with state-wise limits tied to revenue receipts and a warning against the usual March borrowing rush](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/stocks/news/panel-proposes-raising-rbis-ways-and-means-advances-corpus-by-11-2-to-rs-67839-crore/articleshow/134576779.cms). [The panel also urged states to spread market borrowings more evenly across the year](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/industry/banking/rbi-panel-suggests-higher-wma-limit-for-states-flags-year-end-borrowing-126092901286_1.html). Neither touches your wallet directly today, but liquidity withdrawal feeds into what banks pay for deposits and what they charge for loans — the unglamorous machinery that keeps the payments stack cheap enough to run.
