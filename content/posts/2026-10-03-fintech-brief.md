# Fintech Brief — October 3, 2026

## Today's Top Stories
1. UPI volume rose 27% in the first half of FY27, even as the merchant-fee dispute moves toward its October 15 start.
2. NBFCs originated half of India’s new-to-credit borrowers in the June quarter, double their share a decade ago.
3. RBI appointed a new Executive Director to lead supervisory assessment; no new fintech-specific RBI or SEBI rule surfaced in this scan.

---

### 1. UPI clears 145 billion transactions in H1; merchant-fee dispute remains live

UPI processed about 145 billion transactions in April–September, up 27% from 114 billion in the same half of FY26, according to NPCI data reported by *The Economic Times*. Transaction value rose 20% to ₹177 lakh crore from ₹148 lakh crore. These are six-month totals, not a monthly run rate; the figures show the rail’s scale continuing to expand ahead of a policy change that has drawn retailer resistance. [ET’s report](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/upi-volume-rises-27-to-touch-145-billion-in-first-half-of-fy27/articleshow/134642468.cms) cites NPCI data; see also [NPCI’s UPI statistics](https://www.npci.org.in/what-we-do/upi/product-statistics).

The October 2 “No UPI Day” boycott was called off after trade groups met Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman. That does not mean the proposed Merchant Discount Rate has been withdrawn: the current plan is to begin on October 15, charging merchants 0.4% on specified UPI payments above ₹2,000, while person-to-person transfers remain free. The [NPCI FAQ](https://www.npci.org.in/uploads/FA_Qs_Merchant_Discount_Rate_MDR_on_Select_UPI_P2_M_Transactions_58dba1d39e.pdf) describes the framework; [Mint’s report](https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/is-october-2-no-upi-day-what-you-need-to-know-as-trade-bodies-mobile-retailers-call-off-mdr-protest-11790867263571.html) details the protest withdrawal and traders’ requests for changes. The important consumer distinction is that this is a merchant-side charge, not a new fee billed directly to UPI customers. The unresolved question is whether merchants absorb it or try to pass costs on through prices.

### 2. NBFCs now originate half of first-time borrowers

Non-bank finance companies accounted for 50% of new-to-credit originations in the three months ended June 2026, up from 24% in the same period of 2016, according to a TransUnion CIBIL–Finance Industry Development Council study covering about 2,000 NBFCs. “New-to-credit” means a borrower’s first loan reported to the retail credit bureau; it does not mean half of all lending or half of the population. [The Economic Times’ account of the report](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/share-of-new-to-credit-customers-rises-to-50-in-june-2026-from-24-in-june-2016-transunion-cibil-fidc-report/articleshow/134645233.cms) says consumer-durable loans were the largest entry product in January–June 2026, at 30% of new-to-credit originations, followed by personal loans at 12%.

The shift matters because a first loan can create a credit record—and because a mis-sold or unaffordable loan can be a borrower’s first formal financial harm. The study says NBFCs’ share of retail-loan originations by volume rose to 43% from 33% over the decade, while their share by value reached 30%, up from 22%. One important data limitation: CIBIL does not separately observe end-use, so the study treats small loan sizes only as a proxy for phone financing. Better access is meaningful only if borrowers see the full cost, repayment schedule and consequences of missed payments before signing.

### 3. RBI names new head of supervisory assessment

The RBI announced on October 2 that Sudhakar Malli had been appointed Executive Director with effect from October 1. He will lead the Department of Supervision (Supervisory Assessment), after a career spanning roughly three decades, including more than 25 years supervising banks, NBFCs and co-operative banks. [The RBI announcement](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63719) is a leadership change, not a new compliance direction for regulated firms. SEBI’s latest relevant notice found in this scan was its October 1 [Project Jagrook circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2026/display-of-investor-awareness-message-s-by-stock-brokers-on-their-trading-apps-and-websites-under-project-jagrook_104858.html), already covered in yesterday’s brief.
