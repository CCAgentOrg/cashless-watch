## 📰 Sector News & Outlook

- **UPI MDR, from October 15:** a 0.4% merchant discount rate applies to specified person-to-merchant payments above ₹2,000; transactions up to ₹2,000 and person-to-person transfers remain free. The Supreme Court issued notices but declined an interim stay. The fee is levied within the merchant/acquiring ecosystem; do not infer a particular listed company's share or a consumer surcharge without evidence. A survey-based report projected 10% lower UPI value and 4% lower volume after rollout; those are estimates based on stated intentions, not observed post-rollout switching. [^11] [^12]
- **Bank leadership:** HDFC Bank named Anup Bagchi as its next MD & CEO after RBI approval; he is to take charge on October 27. [^5]
- **Brokerage views are not forecasts:** Macquarie upgraded Paytm to Outperform and downgraded PB Fintech to Neutral in its September 30 note. The rating changes do not alter the close data above. [^8]
- **Macro:** GIFT Nifty's positive cue comes after an eighth consecutive weekly decline in the Nifty; crude, bond yields and foreign selling remain key risks. The RBI MPC begins a three-day meeting today, with its decision due October 7; a 25-basis-point hike is economist speculation, not an RBI signal. [^3] [^2] [^13] [^14]

- **SEBI:** Project Jagrook starts on broker websites today. From October 5–31, website messages and existing risk disclosures are mandatory; app display is optional. From November 1, website landing-page placement is mandatory, and broker apps must show both messages and risk disclosures on alternate days. [^15]

### CashlessConsumer lens

The October 15 change is a merchant-side fee framework, not proof of higher earnings for any one payment app. The useful watch is what merchants are charged, what providers actually disclose about fee-sharing, and whether costs are passed on to customers.
