### 1. The RBI rate debate is now about credit resilience as well as inflation

A *Mint* analysis published on October 5 says nine of ten economists in its poll expected a 25-basis-point repo-rate increase later this week. That is a forecast, not an RBI decision. The same column argues that tighter policy may not immediately break the credit upcycle: it points to non-food bank credit growth of at least 14% year on year since December 2025, strong economic growth, ample liquidity and unmet business demand for formal credit. Those factors could cushion a shallow increase, while sustained or sharper tightening could still weaken borrowing and investment, according to [Mint's analysis](https://www.livemint.com/economy/india-credit-growth-rate-hike-rbi-monetary-policy-bank-lending-bank-credit-fcnr-b-deposits-financial-deepening-11791139917638.html).

For digital lenders and NBFC-backed apps, the transmission matters more than the headline alone: wholesale funding can reprice, while bank loans tied to external benchmarks may adjust faster than deposit costs. That does not mean every app's APR changes by 25 basis points on announcement day. Borrowers should check their lender's rate notice and loan terms; a market forecast is not a change to an individual contract. The RBI's statement—not the economist poll—is the decision point.

### 2. SEBI's Project Jagrook website rules are now in force

The first phase of SEBI's October 1 circular began on October 5. Through October 31, stock brokers must display SEBI's investor-awareness messages **and** existing risk disclosures on their websites. App display is voluntary during this phase; if a broker chooses to show a message in its app, the risk disclosure is optional on that day. From November 1, messages must appear on broker website landing pages, and trading apps must show both messages and risk disclosures on their landing pages on alternate days, as set out in [SEBI's circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/oct-2026/1790852162769.pdf).

The distinction is useful for investors: the October phase requires website display, not daily in-app prompts. The circular does not replace the underlying risk disclosures; it adds a prominent investor-education layer. Whether individual brokers have implemented it is a separate question—the rule's start date is not evidence that every site is compliant. SEBI's circular listing still showed the October 1 Project Jagrook notice as its latest circular in the [SEBI circular index](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do?doListing=yes&sid=1&smid=0&ssid=0) checked for this brief.
