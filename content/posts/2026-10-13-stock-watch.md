## 📰 Sector News & Today's Setup

- **RBI liquidity operation:** RBI scheduled a ₹1.5 lakh crore overnight variable-rate reverse repo auction for today, with bids from 9:30–10:00 AM; the one-day placement reverses on October 14. RBI described it as a liquidity operation, not a policy-rate change. [^6]
- **Bank hedging rule:** An RBI direction issued October 12 requires a cash Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve equal to 20% of the rupee-equivalent notional for certain eligible rupee FX hedges on banks' own assets and liabilities. It applies to specified contracts entered after the direction; it is not a UPI or consumer fee. [^7]
- **UPI merchant discount rate watch:** Moneycontrol reported that the October 9 UPI Steering Committee meeting on a possible deferral did not take place and no new decision date was clear. The previously announced 0.4% MDR for eligible merchant transactions above ₹2,000 was still due to start October 15 in that report; a January 2027 delay remained a proposal, not a confirmed change. Treat any new announcement as a potential headline catalyst for payment companies, not as an explanation for Monday's moves. [^8]
