# Fintech Brief — October 24, 2026

## Today's Top Stories
1. RBI fixes April 1, 2027 as the start date for revised operational-risk capital rules for commercial banks.
2. SEBI sets November 8 for a limited relaxation in research analysts’ call-recording rules for institutional clients.
3. FSDC puts fintech innovation, tokenisation, cyber resilience and MSME-focused digital infrastructure on its agenda.

### 1. RBI sets the operational-risk capital start date

The RBI’s October 23 amendment gives banks a firm implementation date for its Basel III standardised approach to operational-risk capital: April 1, 2027. It also supplies an indicative mapping between the Business Indicator used in the calculation and line items in banks’ financial statements, and incorporates clarifications on how some items should be classified. The framework applies to commercial banks, excluding small finance banks, payments banks and regional rural banks. Its components include interest, lease and dividend income; service-related income and expenses; and financial income and losses.

This is a prudential-capital change, not a new customer charge or UPI rule. The practical work now is for banks to map and validate their data and reporting ahead of the start date. The RBI’s [press release](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63858) explains the change; the [amendment directions](https://rbi.org.in/scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13744&Mode=0) set out the formal text.

### 2. SEBI narrows mandatory call recording for institutional research clients

A [SEBI circular dated October 23](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2026/review-of-regulatory-requirement-for-maintenance-of-call-records-of-institutional-clients-by-research-analysts-or-research-entities_105400.html) says the relaxation for research analysts and research entities dealing with institutional investors will take effect on November 8. From then, they will not have to keep audio recordings of those institutional-client calls. The broader duty to preserve a verifiable record of client interactions remains, according to [ANI’s report](https://www.aninews.in/news/business/sebis-call-recording-rules-for-research-analysts-to-come-into-effect-from-november-820261023180312).

The distinction matters: this is not permission to leave research advice undocumented, nor a relaxation for retail clients. The record can take other legally verifiable forms, while the existing obligations for non-institutional clients remain. Investors should still be able to point to a documented interaction if a dispute arises; the change is about the format required for a specific client category, not the standard of conduct.

### 3. FSDC discusses tokenisation, cyber resilience and MSME-facing DPI

At its October 23 meeting, the Financial Stability and Development Council discussed promoting fintech innovation and exploring tokenisation of financial and real-world assets. It also reviewed financial-sector cyber resilience and listed priorities that include simplifying digital KYC and onboarding for NRIs and foreign portfolio investors, and scaling the Account Aggregator and Unified Lending Interface frameworks with a focus on MSMEs. Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman urged regulators to accelerate refunds of unclaimed deposits, dividends, shares, post-office balances, insurance proceeds and pension funds.

These are agenda items and calls for follow-through—not a new tokenisation framework, a launch date for ULI, or a fresh rule for fintechs. The [official PIB account of the meeting](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2327596) also records a decision to strengthen coordination among regulators. For consumers and smaller firms, the test will be whether these discussions produce clear safeguards, usable complaint routes and measurable reductions in friction—not just more digital rails.

## UPI and funding check

No new UPI implementation change surfaced in the NPCI circular and press-release checks for this window. The latest timing signal remains NPCI’s [October 13 statement](https://www.npci.org.in/uploads/UPI_MDR_on_Select_Merchant_Transactions_Shall_be_Implemented_Post_Diwali_4c9cdd77d5.pdf): the MDR framework for selected person-to-merchant transactions is deferred until after Diwali, with the date to be notified separately. The funding scan did not verify a new India fintech-specific round with a disclosed amount and valuation in the same window; broader startup funding news was not counted as fintech.
