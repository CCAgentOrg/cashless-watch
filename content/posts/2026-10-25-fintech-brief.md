# Fintech Brief — October 25, 2026

## Today's Top Stories
1. SEBI outlines a four-part capital-markets roadmap, including tokenised corporate bonds and safeguards for AI and trading terminals.
2. MCA extends FY26 company filing deadlines and waives additional fees for eligible filings.
3. Indian startup funding falls 56% week-on-week; no new fintech venture round with disclosed terms surfaced in the 24-hour window.

### 1. SEBI sketches its next market rulebook: deeper markets, safer technology

In an [October 24 address](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/speeches/oct-2026/address-by-chairman-at-anmi-convention_105415.html) to the Association of National Exchanges Members of India, SEBI chair Tuhin Kanta Pandey set out a four-part agenda: simpler rules, deeper markets, responsible technology and wider investor access. The market is already large—Pandey cited roughly ₹470 lakh crore in equity market value, ₹61 lakh crore in outstanding corporate bonds, ₹83 lakh crore in mutual-fund assets and more than 15 crore unique investors—but participation remains shallow. SEBI’s investor survey, as cited in the speech, found that nearly two-thirds of households know about securities products while fewer than one in ten invest; nearly half of households planning to invest are rural.

The proposals have practical implications for brokers and fintech platforms. SEBI is exploring compliance requirements calibrated to a broker’s scale, client exposure and dependence on technology; developing safeguards against trading-terminal misuse; and exploring an AI sandbox. It is also finalising responsible-AI guidance with tiered accountability, data controls, kill switches and human oversight. On market infrastructure, the chair said SEBI is reviewing short-selling and securities-lending rules and plans for tokenised corporate bonds to trade in the secondary market as part of the next Demat 2.0 phase. These are announced priorities and work in progress—not new rules or a dated launch. [Moneycontrol’s report](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/sebi-plans-safeguards-against-trading-terminal-misuse-graded-compliance-for-brokers-tuhin-kanta-pandey-14059202.html) highlights the broker and technology measures.

### 2. MCA gives companies more time to file FY26 statements and returns

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs has extended the deadline for filing FY26 financial statements through AOC-4 forms, including XBRL and applicable NBFC forms, to December 31, 2026, without additional fees. Annual returns through MGT-7 and MGT-7A now have until January 31, 2027, also without additional fees, according to [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/mca-extends-annual-filing-deadlines-waives-extra-fees-for-fy26-filings-126102401098_1.html), reporting on an October 24 circular.

The extension follows company-secretary concerns about MCA-21 portal downtime, validation failures and other filing problems. It gives fintech firms and NBFCs that file through the system more breathing room, but it is a deadline change—not a waiver of the underlying reporting duties. The report says statutory AGM timelines are unchanged, so companies should not treat the extension as a general compliance holiday.

### 3. Funding cools across startups; fintech-specific deal flow is quiet

Entrackr’s [weekly funding report, published October 24](https://entrackr.com/report/weekly-funding-report/funding-and-acquisitions-in-indian-startups-this-week-oct-19-oct-24-12744844), counted $133.9 million raised by 15 Indian startups in the week ending October 24: four growth-stage deals, ten early-stage deals and one undisclosed round. That was 56% below the previous week’s $302.85 million. AI led by deal count; healthtech, travel, electric mobility, food, space and logistics also featured. The report did not identify a new fintech venture round in that weekly set, and our scan found no India fintech raise with disclosed amount and valuation announced in this 24-hour window. The broad funding slowdown is useful context, but it should not be read as a fintech-specific trend.
