# Fintech Brief — October 27, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### Paytm's profit jumps; the underlying comparison is less dramatic

One 97 Communications, Paytm's parent, reported Q2 FY27 revenue from operations of **₹2,648 crore**, up **28% year on year**, and consolidated profit after tax of **₹225 crore**, against ₹21 crore a year earlier. Merchant GMV rose **26% to ₹7.1 lakh crore**; average monthly transacting users reached **8.5 crore**, up 13%. The company also reported 1.64 crore merchants paying for subscriptions, 19% higher year on year. These operating figures show expansion in payments and merchant monetisation, not just a swing in accounting profit.

The headline profit comparison needs care. The prior-year quarter included a **₹190 crore exceptional loss**; profit before exceptional items rose a more modest **9% to ₹229 crore**. Paytm's reported EBITDA was ₹270 crore, up 90%, while its comparable measure—excluding the prior-year Payments Infrastructure Development Fund incentive—rose from ₹68 crore to ₹258 crore. That is meaningful improvement, but it is a company-defined comparison and should be read alongside the reported figures. [Paytm's exchange-filed earnings release](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachLive/26f2ddb9-d9e7-455e-a49f-5250334f26fe.pdf)

The board separately approved investments of up to **₹500 crore** in Paytm Cloud Technologies and up to **₹500 crore** by that subsidiary in Paytm Singapore over the next year. These are authorised ceilings for working capital, investment and expansion—not a new external fundraise. [Board-meeting filing](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachLive/aae1543b-7f30-4007-b418-b2eda939e0a3.pdf)

### RBI's new Odisha campus puts payments resilience in focus

The RBI's new Greenfield Data Centre and Cybersecurity Training Institute in Khordha, Odisha, was inaugurated on October 25; the announcement remained in Monday's news cycle. The RBI says the facility will strengthen the technological foundation of the financial system. The Finance Minister said it will house mission-critical RBI systems, including RTGS and NEFT, while the institute will build expertise in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence and data centres. [RBI's announcement](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63862) · [Business Today report, October 26](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/story/upi-leads-global-real-time-payments-with-over-2400-cr-monthly-transactions-nirmala-sitharaman-563559-2026-10-26)

The same remarks put UPI's scale at more than **2,400 crore transactions a month**, or nearly 80 crore a day; NPCI data cited by Business Today recorded 24.07 billion transactions in September. That scale makes operational resilience a public-interest issue, not just a back-office concern. The RBI release does not publish the campus's capacity, redundancy design or recovery targets, so the inauguration alone cannot establish how much resilience has improved.

### UPI MDR is still prospective—and Paytm has put a number on its upside

Paytm told the exchanges it expects the proposed merchant discount rate to add about **2.5 basis points** to its overall payment-processing margin, based on the company's current GMV mix. The estimate relates to a **0.4% cap on select person-to-merchant payments above ₹2,000**; Paytm says the effect may take two to three quarters after implementation to stabilise. This is management's estimate, not revenue already earned or a guaranteed outcome.

NPCI's October 13 notice moved implementation to “immediately after Diwali”, with a specific date still to be notified. The notice applies to selected merchant transactions, not every UPI payment. The unresolved date and the eventual merchant mix matter: consumers should not mistake a proposed merchant charge for a universal UPI fee.
