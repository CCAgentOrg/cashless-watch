# Fintech Brief — October 30, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### Supreme Court: banks must repay digital-fraud victims in full when due diligence fails

The Supreme Court on Thursday directed that the RBI's pending **KYC (Amendment) Directions, 2026** must make banks financially liable for digital-arrest scams. A bench of Chief Justice Surya Kant and Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohana, hearing the suo motu case *In Re: Victims of Digital Arrest Related to Forged Documents*, ordered that the framework "must mandate financial institutions to indemnify victims of digital arrest or cyber fraud through suitable compensation, **not less than the amount lost** by the customer, where the fraud or crime is found to have resulted from a failure of due diligence by the financial institution." [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/india/what-is-wrong-with-victims-sc-questions-educated-people-duped-by-digital-arrest-scams-pushes-banks-to-pay-for-losses-14063612.html) · [New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2026/Oct/29/banks-must-compensate-digital-fraud-victims-for-loss-if-due-diligence-failure-established-sc-2)

The court wants the rules to go beyond advisories: temporarily suspending transactions that are unusual for a customer's history, automated suspicious-transaction reporting, and physical verification where warranted. The RBI has been told to submit a list of all authorised financial institutions the framework will cover; the amendments are to be notified by **November 30** and made operational by **December 31, 2026**. [Verdictum](https://www.verdictum.in/supreme-court/rbi-rules-must-banks-compensate-victims-not-less-amount-lost-due-diligence-fails-1624758) · [New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2026/Oct/29/banks-must-compensate-digital-fraud-victims-for-loss-if-due-diligence-failure-established-sc-2)

Scale check: even with a reported ~50% decline in cybercrime, victims have lost about **₹800 crore** to such fraud so far this year. [New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2026/Oct/29/banks-must-compensate-digital-fraud-victims-for-loss-if-due-diligence-failure-established-sc-2)

CJI Kant was blunt in both directions — "unless a penal direction was issued to the banks, they would never mend their ways" ([Verdictum](https://www.verdictum.in/supreme-court/rbi-rules-must-banks-compensate-victims-not-less-amount-lost-due-diligence-fails-1624758)), while also asking what kind of people believe they can get arrested over a mobile phone ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/what-kind-of-people-believe-they-can-get-arrested-on-mobile-cji-expresses-shock-at-digital-arrest-scams-556486)).

Consumer takeaway: if these directions land as written, part of the cost of a mule-account fraud chain shifts to the bank that failed to spot it. The operative word is "established" — expect banks to contest due-diligence findings, so report fraud immediately and keep every screenshot and transaction record.

### Pine Labs posts best growth in five quarters — and says it's MDR-ready

Pine Labs reported **Q2 FY27 net profit of ₹38.57 crore**, up more than sixfold from ₹5.97 crore a year ago, on revenue of **₹806.19 crore, up 24%** — its fastest growth in five quarters. EBITDA rose 67.3% to ₹126 crore, lifting margins to 15.6% from 11.6%. The stock gained as much as 7% intraday to ₹173.80 on the NSE, though it remains down roughly 30% in 2026. [Businessworld](https://www.businessworld.in/article/pine-labs-q2-profit-rises-over-sixfold-to-rs-39-cr-revenue-grows-24-631098) · [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/pine-labs-q2-results-revenue-growth-hits-five-quarter-high-ebitda-rises-share-price-reaction-20022342.htm) · [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/pine-labs-q2-results-net-profit-rises-84-to-nearly-rs-39-crore-shares-up-over-6-14063430.html)

CEO Amrish Rau said both the merchant-acceptance and card-issuing businesses grew, international markets contributed 15% of revenue, and products launched in the past two years now make up 14% of revenue. The company is targeting 21–23% revenue growth at over 20% EBITDA margins. [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/pine-labs-q2-results-net-profit-rises-84-to-nearly-rs-39-crore-shares-up-over-6-14063430.html)

The MDR angle is the one to watch: rollout of the merchant-discount framework has been deferred pending regulatory clarity, and Pine Labs says its distribution network, technology and operations are ready "to support implementation once the final framework and timeline are announced." [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/pine-labs-q2-results-revenue-growth-hits-five-quarter-high-ebitda-rises-share-price-reaction-20022342.htm)

Consumer takeaway: merchants pay MDR, but its deferral is what keeps the IPO valuations of PhonePe, Razorpay and the rest in limbo. When the framework finally lands, watch whether small merchants' QR pricing quietly changes.

### A cheaper FASTag pass for 20-km commuters opens November 1

From **November 1**, non-commercial vehicle owners living within 20 km of a toll plaza can buy the new concessional **FASTag Local Pass**: **₹1,500 a year** at plazas outside municipal limits, **₹3,000** within them. It will be issued at eligible plazas from November 1 and through the Rajmarg Yatra app from December 1. The pass does not apply on closed-system national highways and expressways, or where a service or alternative road exists. It was notified on October 26 under the National Highways Fee (Fifth Amendment) Rules, 2026, with the government free to revise charges every April 1. [Livemint](https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/new-fastag-local-pass-from-1-nov-costs-coverage-validity-rules-compared-with-annual-pass-which-one-should-you-buy-11793245719649.html) · [Mashable India](https://in.mashable.com/automobiles/115503/fastag-annual-local-pass-gets-government-approval-check-price-eligibility-and-benefits)

Consumer takeaway: for daily local trips that were billed full toll each crossing, this is real money back — but eligibility hinges on the 20-km residence rule and the excluded road types, so read the fine print before buying.
