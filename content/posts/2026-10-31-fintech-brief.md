# Fintech Brief — October 31, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### RBI's FERR framework is quietly raising the cost of forex hedging

The Reserve Bank's new **Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve (FERR)** framework, effective for transactions from October 12, requires banks to park cash equal to **20% of the notional value** of eligible rupee-linked FX derivative contracts with the RBI — where it earns no interest. It covers contracts users take to hedge current-account transactions above $2 million notional, plus banks' own balance-sheet currency risk, and restricts rebooking of cancelled contracts. [Rediff/Business Standard](https://www.rediff.com/business/report/rbis-ferr-framework-how-new-rules-impact-fcnrb-hedging-costs-for-banks/20261030.htm)

The first visible price is in the forward market: the 12-month dollar-rupee premium has climbed **102 basis points in October, from 3.36% at end-September to 4.38%** on Thursday. The squeeze is sharpest on **FCNR(B)** deposits — banks raised a much larger-than-expected **$133 billion** through the RBI's special swap window (closed a month early on August 31), but the swap covers only principal, leaving banks to hedge interest payments themselves at now-elevated cost. [Rediff/Business Standard](https://www.rediff.com/business/report/rbis-ferr-framework-how-new-rules-impact-fcnrb-hedging-costs-for-banks/20261030.htm)

Consumer takeaway: hedging costs eventually flow into deposit rates on FCNR(B) accounts and import-priced goods. If you hold an FCNR(B) deposit, watch what your bank offers at renewal.

### SEBI gives low-risk foreign investors an eight-year KYC cycle

SEBI has relaxed KYC norms for foreign portfolio investors: **Category I FPIs from non-high-risk jurisdictions will now undergo periodic KYC review once every eight years**, instead of every one or three years, and certain registration-related compliance requirements have been removed. The circular takes effect **December 1, 2026**, and is framed as ease-of-doing-business paperwork relief for the foreign money that underwrites Indian markets. [ET LegalWorld](http://legal.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/regulators/sebi-eases-kyc-norms-for-foreign-investors-extends-review-cycle-to-eight-years-for-eligible-fpis/135372988)

Consumer takeaway: this is institutional plumbing, but it matters — smoother FPI onboarding is aimed at deeper market liquidity. The eight-year gap only applies to the lowest-risk category, so the high-risk-jurisdiction scrutiny (the FPI beneficial-ownership concern SEBI has chased for years) stays in place.

### UPI goes live for online bookings at Burj Khalifa and Resorts World Sentosa

Indian travellers can now pay with UPI apps to book **At the Top, Burj Khalifa** — the first UAE attraction to accept UPI for e-commerce, enabled by NIPL's partnership with **NEOPAY and Emaar Entertainment** — and **Resorts World Sentosa** in Singapore (Universal Studios Singapore, the Singapore Oceanarium, Adventure Cove Waterpark) through a tie-up with **FOMO Pay**. The finance ministry announced both integrations went live on Friday. [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/nri/latest-updates/upi-payments-expanded-to-online-bookings-at-tourist-attractions-in-uae-singapore/articleshow/135369519.cms) · [ANI](https://www.aninews.in/news/business/upi-payments-expanded-to-online-bookings-at-tourist-attractions-in-uae-singapore20261030205122)

UPI is now accepted across **10 countries** — Singapore, UAE, France, Mauritius, Nepal, Bhutan, Qatar, Sri Lanka, Cambodia and Greece — with Uzbekistan lined up as the 11th. [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/nri/latest-updates/upi-payments-expanded-to-online-bookings-at-tourist-attractions-in-uae-singapore/articleshow/135369519.cms)

Consumer takeaway: booking foreign attractions over UPI means no forex markup on the ticket — but check your app's cross-border terms, and remember a foreign-issued refund would still land outside UPI.

### Navi's $100 million Prosus round clears CCI ahead of IPO

The Competition Commission of India has approved **Prosus's $100 million (~₹1,000 crore) investment in Sachin Bansal's Navi**, via Prosus subsidiary MIH Payments Holdings BV for a minority stake — the fintech's **first external institutional raise** since its 2018 founding, coming months ahead of a potential **~$2 billion IPO** (the investment reportedly landed at the same valuation). [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/navi-gets-cci-nod-for-100-million-prosus-investment-ahead-of-ipo-article-14010565.html) · [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/navi-100m-prosus-investment)

Scale check: Prosus says Navi is India's **fourth-largest UPI app**, processing ~$5.05 billion across 947 million transactions in July, with lending AUM of roughly ₹13,000 crore in FY26 and consolidated profitability in Q4. [FinTech Futures](https://www.fintechfutures.com/venture-capital-funding/navi-100m-prosus-investment)

- **Crypto — Binance hack reaches Indian exchanges:** Binance estimates the impact of Thursday's breach — apparently via a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop — at **$6.3 billion, the largest crypto theft on record**, with ~$3 billion frozen on-chain and losses to be covered by SAFU and company funds; investigators link it to a North Korean group. The India spillover: Binance is the biggest liquidity provider behind futures trading on major Indian exchanges, and **CoinDCX has pushed futures into reduce-only mode** (customers can't open or add positions; deposits and withdrawals are unaffected).
