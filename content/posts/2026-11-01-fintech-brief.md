# Fintech Brief — November 1, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### Centre constitutes a banking reform super-committee — with the RBI at the table

The Finance Ministry has constituted the **High Level Committee on Banking for Viksit Bharat**, a panel charged with recommending **structural, institutional, regulatory and legislative reforms** to make Indian banks more globally competitive, financially inclusive and resilient. It will be chaired by former Central Vigilance Commissioner **Suresh N. Patel** (earlier MD & CEO of Andhra Bank), and its named members include sitting **RBI Deputy Governor Shirish Chandra Murmu**, former Indian Bank MD & CEO **Padmaja Chunduru**, and former RBI Deputy Governor **N. S. Vishwanathan** — with the Joint Secretary (Banking) in DFS as member secretary. First announced by Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman in the Union Budget 2026-27, the committee has **six months, until April 2027**, to report. [Rediff](https://www.rediff.com/business/report/former-cvc-suresh-n-patel-to-chair-high-level-committee-on-banking-reforms-for-viksit-bharat/20261031.htm) · [Nation Path India](https://www.nationpathindia.com/economy/centre-constitutes-high-level-banking-committee-viksit-bharat-2026)

Why it matters: a former RBI Deputy Governor and a serving Deputy Governor reviewing the *regulatory* framework together is a notable configuration — this is the closest look at banking regulation's own architecture since the post-NPA reform era, and anything it recommends on governance, consolidation or the RBI's supervisory toolkit will eventually reach depositors and borrowers as product and pricing changes.

### RBI demands proof of cybersecurity compliance after a run of hacks

The Reserve Bank has **stepped up scrutiny of banks' cybersecurity preparedness**, asking lenders to demonstrate compliance with existing security requirements, investigate vulnerabilities in **APIs connecting banks with NBFCs and fintech partners**, and explain their readiness for **AI-related threats** — following incidents including a suspected data exposure of about **3,900 customers at State Bank of India** and unauthorised transfers at other major lenders. An **October 19 RBI advisory** went further, asking regulated entities to **treat their technology environments as compromised until forensically cleared**. CERT-In has meanwhile offered training to officials from numerous financial institutions. [Economic Times/Reuters](https://m.economictimes.com/news/economy/policy/rbi-cracks-down-on-cybersecurity-banks-face-increased-scrutiny-amid-rising-ai-driven-hacks/articleshow/135352566.cms) · [ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/cybersecurity-becomes-a-balance-sheet-risk-for-lenders-as-breaches-rise/135378116) · [The 420](https://the420.in/policy-initiative/rbi-seeks-proof-of-cybersecurity-compliance-from-banks-after-recent-hacks)

Consumer takeaway: the bank–fintech API layer is where your KYC and account data actually lives; if you use a lending or payments app, the questions RBI is now asking your bank are about *your* data. After a breach, you're entitled to prompt notification and remediation — and this week's Supreme Court push means banks that fail basic due diligence are moving toward strict liability for fraud losses.

### PB Fintech's Q2 profit jumps 43% — under an IRDAI commission-reform cloud

**PB Fintech** (Policybazaar/Paisabazaar) reported Q2 FY27 consolidated net profit of **₹192.5 crore, up 42.7%** from ₹134.9 crore a year ago, on revenue of **₹2,178 crore, up 35%**. Total insurance premium facilitated grew **38% to ₹9,385 crore**, core renewal-and-trail revenue rose **42% to ₹1,097 crore** on a 12-month rolling basis, and Paisabazaar disbursals have now grown sequentially for five straight quarters. [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/pb-fintech-s-net-profit-rises-43-to-rs-192-crore-in-q2-revenue-up-35-to-rs-2-178-crore-article-14064621.html) · [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/business/india-business/pb-fintech-q2-results-profit-rises-43-to-rs-192-crore-revenue-grows-35/articleshow/135364693.cms) · [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/stocks/pb-fintech-share-price-q2-results-net-profit-jumps-as-new-protection-premium-core-insurance-revenue-surge-20023618.htm)

The catch: management is bracing for the regulator's proposed **insurance commission reforms**, which could touch more than a third of revenue — Policybazaar plans to respond by tightening cost control and growing non-insurance revenue. [Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/tech/technology/pb-fintech-q2-profit-rises-43-to-rs-192-crore-revenue-grows-35/articleshow/135363912.cms) Consumer takeaway: commission caps are meant to lower distribution costs baked into your premium — but watch that savings don't come out of advice quality instead.
