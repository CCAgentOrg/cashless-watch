# Fintech Brief — November 5, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### NPCI spins up a tech subsidiary to chase AI and blockchain

The **National Payments Corporation of India has set up NPCI Tech Solutions Limited (NTSL)**, a wholly owned technology and innovation subsidiary announced November 4 — its **fourth**, after NPCI International Payments, NPCI Bharat BillPay and NPCI BHIM Services. NTSL will work on **AI, blockchain, digital financial infrastructure and global capability centre services**, building capabilities beyond NPCI's core payments mandate and providing tech services for several of **India's digital public infrastructure programmes**. NPCI's chief technology officer **Vishal Kanvaty**, with the organisation for nearly a decade, moves over as **MD and CEO**. "Advancements in AI and other emerging technologies are creating new possibilities across digital infrastructure and financial services," said NPCI CEO **Dilip Asbe**. [Reuters via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/indias-payments-authority-npci-forms-110215361.html)

Consumer takeaway: the operator of UPI is formally institutionalising AI and blockchain work outside its non-profit payments core. That could mean better fraud detection and smarter infrastructure — but a separate commercial entity selling tech services also blurs the line between the cooperative that runs the country's payment rails and a vendor competing in the market those rails enable. Watch how NTSL's revenue and governance are disclosed.

### UPI notches record October — even as bank-side failures creep up

**UPI crossed ₹30 lakh crore in monthly transaction value for the first time** and processed **25.47 billion transactions in October** — an average of **821 million a day**, with **average daily value topping ₹1 lakh crore for the first time**. The previous monthly value record was ₹29.9 lakh crore in May 2026. The milestone lands just as the ecosystem braces for **MDR charges on higher-value UPI merchant payments, deferred until after Diwali (November 8)** on a date NPCI is yet to notify. [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/upi-s-average-daily-transaction-value-tops-rs-1-lakh-crore-for-first-time-hits-monthly-records-article-14065352.html) · [PaymentsJournal](https://www.paymentsjournal.com/upi-sets-daily-transaction-record-but-growth-faces-headwinds)

The record comes against a sobering quality signal: NPCI's September data shows **technical decline rates among the 20 largest remitter banks rose to 0.24%, from 0.18% in August**. Four banks saw their rates more than double — **ICICI Bank at 0.73% (from 0.03%)**, **SBI at 0.38% (from 0.10%)**, **Kotak Mahindra at 0.36% (from 0.02%)** and **Indian Overseas Bank at 0.24% (from 0.06%)**. Business declines — wrong PINs, limit breaches — edged up from 10.85% to 10.88%. [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-upi-technical-decline-rate-rises-september-2026)

Consumer takeaway: at 25 billion transactions a month, even a 0.24% technical failure rate means tens of millions of failed payments — money debited or stuck, refunds owed. If a payment fails on ICICI, SBI, Kotak or IOB, it's more likely the bank's systems than your app; auto-refunds for debits are mandatory within T+1, so chase your bank, not the merchant.

### RBI stands up a financial markets committee led by its deputy governor

RBI has constituted the **Technical Consultative Committee for Financial Markets (TCCFM)**, announced November 4 in pursuance of its October 7 Statement on Developmental and Regulatory Policies. The panel — chaired by **Deputy Governor Rohit Jain**, who oversees the Financial Markets Regulation Department — brings **seven external members** including former **SEBI whole-time member Ananth Narayan** and **Ridham Desai**, Morgan Stanley's chief market strategist for India. Its terms of reference cover the **functioning, efficiency, liquidity and resilience** of the money, government securities, foreign exchange and related derivative markets, plus **trading, clearing, settlement and reporting infrastructure**. The committee will meet **at least once every quarter**. [RBI press release](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63914) · [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/story/rbi-names-ridham-desai-ananth-narayan-to-new-financial-markets-panel-on-forex-bond-and-derivatives-markets-565593-2026-11-04)

Consumer takeaway: this committee shapes the plumbing behind your mutual funds, bonds and forex remittances. Institutionalising market-participant voice is welcome — but with bankers and market intermediaries advising their own regulator, the disclosures around committee recommendations and conflicts of interest will matter as much as its output.

### Equitas SFB names an interim CEO after Vasudevan exits

**Equitas Small Finance Bank announced (November 3) that MD & CEO P N Vasudevan has resigned**, with whole-time director **Balaji Nuthalapadi appointed interim CEO**, subject to RBI approval. Vasudevan — the bank's founder-CEO, who once attempted to step down in 2022 for philanthropy — leaves after the bank crossed **₹1 lakh crore in total business** and posted its highest-ever quarterly profit of **₹218 crore** in the September quarter. The stock rose over **9.5%** on the announcement. [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/equitas-sfb-shares-rise-over-9-5-as-lender-announces-balaji-nuthalapadi-as-interim-ceo-14068871.html)

Consumer takeaway: leadership churn at a small finance bank is worth watching because these banks hold your deposits under thinner capital buffers than universal banks. Deposits remain insured up to ₹5 lakh by DICGC, and a 9.5% stock pop suggests the market reads continuity, not crisis — but watch for the RBI's approval of the interim arrangement and the permanent successor.
