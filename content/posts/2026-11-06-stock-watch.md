## 📰 Pre-market Setup & Sector News

- **The AI rout India inherits:** Wall Street's best post-midterm rally in 44 years lasted exactly one day. On Thursday, the Nasdaq fell ~5.4% and the S&P 500 ~4.4% — the worst day since April 2025 — as roughly $3 trillion of market value evaporated in the AI/chip complex: Qualcomm −11% (smartphone-chip miss), Arm −11%, Tesla −10% at the lows. Asia ran with it: Kospi −5.28% (trading halts), Taiwan −3.2%, Nikkei −1.79% — and on Friday morning Japan's Nikkei fell more than 6% at the open, with Korea/Taiwan futures down ~7%. Bloomberg's tape: "Asian Stocks Brace for Selloff as AI Rout Deepens." The read-through: Democrats' full Congress sweep is now being priced as regulatory risk to AI data-center capex — the same theme that hit Indian IT on Wednesday before it bounced Thursday. GIFT Nifty signalled a weak open pre-market; the question is whether India's domestic, financials-driven bid decouples for a second straight day.[^6][^7][^8]
- **Nifty levels for the gap-down:** Moneycontrol's trading plan frames the day — on a fade, supports at 24,300–24,200, then 24,000 as the crucial line; on recovery, resistance at 24,800, then the round 25,000. After a +576-point day, that's a wide but honest bracket.[^3]
- **UPI MDR — the sector's binary is now a matter of days:** NPCI's 0.4% MDR on select P2M transactions above ₹2,000 is committed for "immediately post Diwali, at a date to be notified separately." The calendar: Diwali Laxmi Pujan falls Sunday, November 8 (Muhurat trading 2:20–3:30 PM) and the market is shut Tuesday, November 10 (Balipratipada) — so the notification plausibly lands any session from today through next week. Not yet notified as of this morning.[^9][^10]
- **Soundbox rent vs MDR — the business-model fight begins:** Business Standard's Friday-morning piece lays out the dilemma for BharatPe, Google Pay, MobiKwik, Paytm, PhonePe and Pine Labs — merchants won't pay both a ~₹125–130 monthly soundbox rent AND MDR basis points, so providers must pick one monetisation. Pine Labs CEO Amrish Rau, on the Q2 call: "we rather remain on the transaction instead of the base soundboxes number." That's the first concrete operator positioning on post-MDR soundbox economics — a margin story for Paytm (largest soundbox base) and Pine Labs when the notification lands.[^11]
- **Flows:** FIIs bought ₹14,974 crore on Thursday — a fifth straight session (the streak total is now roughly ₹35,800 crore over five days) — while DIIs sold ₹10,444 crore. Foreign money driving a Diwali rally with domestic institutions fading it is a fragile construction if today's global risk-off deepens.[^4]
- **Oil's whipsaw:** Brent crashed ~11% to a $93.50 low Thursday on the midterm read-through (end-of-war hopes), rebounded over 1% late — watch the $90s range; it was the single biggest macro tailwind for Thursday's rally.[^12][^5]
- **Results and filings today (Friday):** Bharti Airtel, LIC, Titan, ONGC, Bharti Hexacom, Glenmark Pharma and Astral are among Q2 FY27 reporters in focus. M&M's Thursday print (Q2 consolidated profit +25% YoY to ₹4,588 crore) fuelled the auto-led leg; financials' earnings season is effectively done — the sector now trades on the MDR and credit narrative, not results.[^13][^5]
- **SEBI keeps plumbing:** the board approved net settlement of cash-market trades for mutual funds from April 1, 2027 — operationally meaningful for the institution-heavy financial complex, market-structure noise for everyone else.[^14]
- **Diwali-week calendar:** Muhurat trading Sunday November 8, 2:20–3:30 PM (pre-open 2:05 PM); market closed Tuesday November 10 (Balipratipada). Position sizing into an MDR-notification window in thin holiday books is its own risk.[^9][^10]
