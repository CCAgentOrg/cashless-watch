## 📰 Pre-market Setup & Sector News

- **UPI MDR — not yet notified; the window opens today:** Diwali (November 8) has passed, and NPCI's committed "immediately post-Diwali" go-live for the 0.4% MDR on select P2M transactions above ₹2,000 (capped ₹300) still has no notified date as of this morning. The notification can land any session now — it remains the payments complex's binary for the week.[^8]
- **NPCI defends the MDR at the Supreme Court:** in affidavits filed November 3–5 in the Anjan Datta PIL, NPCI said the MDR touches only ~4% of P2M transactions above ₹2,000 (96% unaffected; 70% of UPI is P2P and exempt), that small merchants with up to ₹1 lakh monthly UPI QR intake stay at zero MDR, that 5% of MDR collections fund a small-merchant adoption pool — and that merchants **cannot pass the charge on to customers**. The Court had refused a stay on September 28; the challenge now turns on Section 10A's amended no-charge scope (Ministry of Finance notification, September 14).[^9][^10][^11]
- **RBI's new Cyber Resilience directive (November 5):** the RBI issued its cyber resilience framework for regulated entities — an operational-compliance cost line for every bank and payments player on this page, and a fresh procurement cycle for the security vendors behind them.[^12]
- **Pine Labs goes bigger on soundboxes ahead of MDR:** 1 million UPI soundboxes planned, ₹33,000–34,000 crore monthly UPI GTV targeted by FY28, current GTV ₹4.5 lakh crore, H2 FY27 revenue growth guided 23–23.5%. CFO Sameer Kamath says monetisation of the volume has begun. This is the first concrete capacity bet on post-MDR soundbox economics since the go-live was deferred.[^13][^14]
- **AvenuesAI Q2:** profit +6% YoY as payments TPV climbs — steady, but Friday's −3.74% says the market wanted the breakout more than the numbers.[^12]
- **Paytm's sell-side moment:** JP Morgan's "First Take: All-round beat" credits the merchant franchise plus cost control for EBIT breakeven — the first big-bank endorsement since regulatory restrictions were resolved. With PAYTM ~3% from its 52-week high, momentum and narrative are aligned; the MDR notification is the swing factor either way.[^15]
- **Flows — the engine and its counterweight:** FIIs bought ₹13,750 crore Friday, a 14th straight session, taking cumulative inflows to ₹53,154 crore since October 16 (₹14,975 crore on November 5 was the biggest single-day haul in over a year). DIIs sold ₹5,969 crore Friday and ₹24,197 crore for the week. Expectations of a possible LTCG cut for foreign investors are part of the FII story — unconfirmed, and thus a withdrawal risk.[^6][^7]
- **The Nvidia overhang:** Nvidia disclosed an investigation into CEO Jensen Huang over alleged links with a Chinese spy; Trump called Huang "honorable." Nasdaq futures fell as much as 150 points on the news Sunday evening — after Thursday's rout put the Nasdaq 12% under its October 6 record. India's IT complex (Nifty IT +0.43% Friday, the laggard of the four indices here) is the direct read-through; a fresh chip-led drawdown caps the sector again, an Asia bargain-hunt (Nikkei +2%, Taiex +3%) supports it.[^3][^16]
- **Macro calendar:** blockbuster October US jobs report keeps a December Fed hike in play; Thursday's US CPI is the week's directional cue. Rupee closed Friday at 93.89, up 52 paise. Brent below $100. Diwali week continues — the market is shut Tuesday, November 10 (Balipratipada), so effectively a four-day week with the MDR notification hanging over it.[^1][^5]
