## 📰 Holiday + Sector News

- **Markets shut today, reopen Wednesday:** NSE and BSE are closed Tuesday, November 10 for Diwali-Balipratipada across cash, derivatives, EGR and debt segments. Regular trading resumes **Wednesday, November 11, 09:15 IST**. Remaining 2026 holidays: November 24 (Guru Nanak Jayanti) and December 25.[^4][^5]
- **Bank-branch holiday is regional:** RBI's calendar closes branches today only in Ahmedabad, Belapur, Bengaluru, Dehradun, Gangtok, Mumbai and Nagpur — a payments-operations footnote, not a nationwide bank holiday.[^6]
- **UPI MDR — still unnotified as the "post-Diwali" window opens:** NPCI's committed immediately-post-Diwali go-live for the 0.3–0.4% MDR on P2M UPI transactions above ₹2,000 (RuPay debit and sub-₹2,000 UPI exempt) had still not been notified as of the holiday. It can land before Wednesday's open — it remains the payments complex's binary.[^7][^8]
- **NPCI's Supreme Court defence:** in the Anjan Datta PIL affidavits, NPCI called the MDR a commercial charge (not a tax), said merchants and apps **cannot pass it to consumers**, and noted ~96% of P2M transactions are unaffected; the Court had refused a stay on September 28.[^8]
- **Banks plan to reinvest MDR proceeds:** SBI, Axis and Bank of Baroda have told the market the levy's gains go back into technology, security, infrastructure and merchant acquisition — an early read on where the MDR money lands.[^9]
- **Wednesday's earnings overhang:** Q2 prints due today — Kalyan Jewellers, MRF, Cochin Shipyard, Avanti Feeds and others — will be digested at Wednesday's open instead.[^10]
- **Global cues for the reopen:** the rupee closed Monday at 93.93 (among Asia's top-3 performers as India trades as an "AI hedge"), Brent slid ~5% to $93 on Iran peace hopes, and the US October jobs beat (165k, unemployment 4.1%) has a December Fed *hike* in play — Thursday's US CPI is the next global gate.[^11][^12]
