# Fintech Brief — November 11, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### Supreme Court: banks that skip due diligence must pay digital-arrest fraud victims

India's top court has turned compensation for "digital arrest" victims from an RBI suggestion into a liability. In an order uploaded Monday in the suo motu case *In Re: Victims of Digital Arrest Related to Forged Documents*, a bench led by Chief Justice Surya Kant with Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohana directed that financial institutions must compensate victims where fraud succeeds because of a due-diligence failure, described banks as a "protective" shield for account holders, and asked the RBI to submit a list of all authorised financial institutions before the next hearing on November 23 ([Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/digital-arrest-scams-sc-seeks-prevention-plan-from-whatsapp-others-sends-notice-to-rbi/articleshow/135708591.cms); [New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2026/Nov/10/supreme-court-tells-banks-to-pay-victims-for-cyber-fraud-losses-if-found-negligent); [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/supreme-court-directs-whatsapp-apprise-court-measures-prevent-digital-arrest-scam-cybercrimes-557976)). The court said the RBI's proposed framework must carry enforceable provisions for compensation, preventive intervention and institutional accountability — not just advisories. WhatsApp and other intermediaries must also submit their prevention proposals to the court and the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C); WhatsApp said it has acted against 9,400 accounts and is building software to flag suspicious ones ([Rediff/PTI](https://www.rediff.com/news/report/sc-directs-whatsapp-banks-on-digital-arrest-cybercrime-prevention/20261110.htm)). The court noted cybercrimes have fallen roughly 50% this year, but victims have still lost about ₹800 crore.

Consumer takeaway: the "your parcel is detained, pay a security deposit" call now has a price tag attached to bank negligence — but you still have to report promptly. File at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930 within the golden hour, keep call recordings and payment proofs, and remember no genuine agency collects fines over a video call.

### Diwali data: a record day-before-Diwali volume, near-record value on Dhanteras

NPCI's festive numbers released November 10 show UPI at **916.9 million transactions on Diwali (November 8), up 32%** from 695.7 million a year earlier, after a **record 975.8 million transactions the previous day** worth ₹1,36,118 crore ([The Hindu BusinessLine/PTI](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/money-and-banking/upi-transactions-rise-32-on-diwali-2026-to-9169-million/article71686144.ece); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/upi-volume-jumps-32-on-diwali-driven-by-last-minute-festival-buying-126111000636_1.html)). Dhanteras (November 6) logged **₹1.37 lakh crore — the second-highest daily transaction value on record** ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/startups/upi-volume-value-post-strong-growth-during-diwali-as-festive-spending-lights-up-digital-payments/articleshow/135688534.cms)). Across the four-day festive window of November 5–8, UPI handled **3.75 billion transactions worth ₹4.99 lakh crore — up 28% in volume and 38% in value year-on-year**. Value growing faster than volume means bigger average tickets: high-value festival purchases that once went on cards or cash are settling on UPI, which is now accepted in 11 countries.

Consumer takeaway: festive peaks are exactly when fraud traffic peaks too — NPCI's own festive advisory warns against paying on external UPI IDs or links sent outside the shopping app. Stick to scanning at the merchant's own counter, and if a payment above ₹2,000 feels rushed, remember the fee debate below touches only that slice.

### UPI MDR: Diwali came and went, the fee did not

The UPI and Services Steering Committee decided the new merchant discount rate on eligible person-to-merchant UPI transactions — **0.4% on payments above ₹2,000, capped at ₹300 for payments of ₹75,000 and above** — would begin "immediately after Diwali", with the effective date to be notified separately. As of November 10, NPCI has notified no date, and the Financial Express reported sources pointing to a possible deferral to January 1, 2027 — unconfirmed ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/upi-charges-after-diwali-will-customers-pay-extra-on-payments-above-2-000-npcis-mdr-rollout-likely-in-nov-11794289795387.html); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/economy/story/upi-payments-above-rs2000-to-attract-04-mdr-after-diwali-who-pays-who-is-exempt-and-when-charges-begin-566737-2026-11-10)). Merchants heading into Bhai Dooj (November 11) say they still have no clarity for pricing and reconciliation, while the Finance Ministry maintains roughly **96% of UPI merchant transactions will remain unaffected**.

Consumer takeaway: nothing changes at checkout until a date is actually notified — and when it does, the fee is on the merchant's side of the counter; NPCI has told the Supreme Court merchants cannot pass it to customers. If anyone asks you to "pay UPI charges extra", that is a business choice, not a rule.

### Blackstone leads a ₹6,414 crore primary capital raise in IIFL Finance

IIFL Finance's board approved on November 10 a **₹6,414 crore all-primary preferential issue**: **₹5,000 crore (~$535 million) from Blackstone-managed funds** via compulsorily convertible preference shares that convert at ₹602 per share within 18 months for an **approximately 16% fully diluted stake**, alongside ₹1,264 crore from co-promoters Nirmal Jain and R. Venkataraman through warrants and ₹150 crore from Anchorage Capital ([The Hindu BusinessLine/Reuters](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/money-and-banking/blackstone-to-buy-about-16-stake-in-iifl-finance-for-5000-crore/article71685978.ece); [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/blackstone-to-invest-rs-5000-crore-in-iifl-finance-for-16-stake-as-company-raises-rs-6414-crore/articleshow/135711207.cms); [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/companies/blackstone-iifl-finance-promoters-to-invest-rs-6-414-crore-in-nbfc-14076344.html)). The infusion lifts the lender's capital adequacy ratio from about 17% to roughly 30% on a pro forma September 2026 basis, earmarked for growth in secured lending — particularly gold finance — and is being billed as one of the largest primary private equity investments in a listed Indian NBFC. Analysts quoted by Mint expect a stronger capital base to improve credit ratings and cut borrowing costs by 100–150 basis points over time.

Consumer takeaway: private equity crowding into gold-backed retail lending is a bet that households will keep pledging jewellery for credit. Competition can bring cheaper and faster loans, but also aggressive doorstep solicitation — compare the effective annual rate and total interest across lenders before pledging, and keep the auction-notice rights in mind if repayment slips.
