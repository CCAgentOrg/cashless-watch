- **Reopen-day cue — muted, consolidation expected:** GIFT Nifty ~25,326–25,337 this morning (flat to +0.03%) after Tuesday's holiday; analysts see profit-booking after the 10.5% seven-session climb, with global cues — Nvidia, US CPI — setting the direction.[^2][^3][^8]
- **UPI MDR — the date watch continues:** NPCI committed to a go-live "immediately post Diwali" but has **still not notified the date**. A Financial Express report cites a possible deferral to January 1, 2027; NPCI hasn't confirmed either a November date or the slip. The framework: 0.3–0.4% MDR on P2M UPI above ₹2,000 (capped ₹300 for ₹75,000+), RuPay debit and sub-₹2,000 UPI exempt, ~96% of P2M volume untouched.[^4][^5]
- **NPCI's Supreme Court defence (Nov 3–5 filings):** in the Anjan Datta PIL, NPCI called the MDR a commercial charge needed to sustain the ecosystem, said merchants and apps **cannot pass it to consumers**, and relied on the finance ministry's September 14 notification — UPI up to ₹2,000 keeps zero-charge protection, so above-₹2,000 transactions fall outside the statutory bar. The Court had refused a stay on September 28.[^6]
- **Banks pledge to reinvest MDR gains:** SBI (chairman C S Setty: "should not be viewed as a revenue stream"), Axis and Bank of Baroda say proceeds fund technology, security and merchant acquisition — an early read on where the money lands.[^7]
