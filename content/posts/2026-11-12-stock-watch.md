## 📰 Sector News

- **UPI MDR — still no date, and the queue to delay grows:** two days past Deepavali, NPCI has not notified the go-live; its spokesperson told The Hindu "there is no announcement on this at the moment." MediaNama separately reports merchant bodies and fintechs have asked NPCI to push the rollout further. The framework remains 0.3–0.4% on P2M UPI above ₹2,000 (capped ₹300 for ₹75,000+), RuPay debit and sub-₹2,000 UPI exempt, ~96% of P2M volume untouched — but the sector's biggest pricing event in UPI's history is now officially in limbo.[^5][^6]
- **Who wins when it lands — the management math:** Paytm an estimated **₹700 crore annual windfall**; Pine Labs management guiding to **₹80–230 crore a year** in new UPI-MDR-linked revenue. That is Wednesday's bid under PINELABS (+2.74%) and the quiet ₹1,793 PAYTM hold near highs.[^6]
- **NPCI builds a tech company:** NPCI Tech Solutions Ltd (NTSL) — its fourth wholly-owned subsidiary after NIPL, NBBL and NBSL — is live, with CTO Vishal Kanvaty as MD & CEO (announced Nov 4–5, RBI-approved). Focus: AI, blockchain, digital financial infrastructure and GCC services, plus tech services for eKYC Setu and the Unified Ticketing Platform. Read-across for NPST: NPCI is now an in-house competitor for infrastructure-adjacent tech services.[^7]
- **NPCI's Supreme Court defence, quantified:** its Nov 5 affidavit says the MDR touches only ~4% of P2M transactions (above ₹2,000); QR merchants with ≤₹1 lakh monthly UPI turnover stay at zero MDR; 5% of MDR collections fund small-merchant adoption; and merchants cannot pass the charge to consumers — a "mandatory direction backed by contractual enforcement mechanisms."[^8][^9]
- **Pine Labs × Stripe:** unified commerce launched in Singapore (Nov 4) — Stripe for online, Pine Labs terminals in-store, one transaction view. A first template for the merchant-stack consolidation pitch.[^10]
- **Paisabazaar widens the funnel:** instant personal loans expanded to 18 lenders; Diwali Paisa Vouchers cover 300+ brands — PB Fintech's credit-distribution flywheel keeps compounding while the stock sits at half its high.[^11]
- **I4C's warning to fintechs:** India Cyber Crime Coordination Centre flagged AI-agent attacks on payment APIs — agentic commerce now has a threat model.[^11]
- **Flows and fundings:** Blackstone is putting ₹5,000 crore into IIFL Finance (stock +6% Wednesday); bank deposit growth has accelerated past 17% YoY on FCNR-B inflows; PSU banks' November gains have run to ~11%. Bernstein's year-end Nifty target moves to 26,100.[^3][^12][^13]
- **Earnings today:** HAL, Vodafone Idea, IRCTC, RVNL, Hitachi Energy, Oil India, Page Industries, Bharat Dynamics, Alkem Labs, Aurobindo — with Bajaj Finance in watchlists after a red Wednesday.[^14][^15]
