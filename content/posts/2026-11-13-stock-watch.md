## 📰 Sector News

- **CPI breaches the RBI ceiling — rate-hike odds rise:** October retail inflation printed **6.09%** (provisional, new 2024 base year), up from 5.54% in September — above the 6% upper tolerance band for the first time in two years and the first breach on the new series. Food inflation hit 8.94% (onions +116% YoY); rural 6.63% vs urban 5.41%. Economists now see 25 bp hikes at both the December and February reviews; ICRA's Aditi Nayar expects food inflation to stay above 8% in November. RBI has already raised its FY26 forecast to 5.2% and expects the Oct–Dec quarter to average 6%; MPC minutes show policymakers open to further hikes. For banks: margin tailwind, growth question mark — watch how Bank Nifty trades the news it closed 246 points from its record just hours before.[^5][^6][^7]
- **Nvidia loses Jensen Huang — the IT overhang resolves, messily:** Huang resigned as CEO and director effective immediately Thursday; CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO and Harvey C. Jones the first-ever board chair, after revelations of extramarital affairs including one with a woman US intelligence assessed as a Chinese spy. NVDA fell ~9–10% Thursday and NASDAQ closed −0.49%, but Asia is stabilising this morning (Nikkei +1.54%) and Nvidia's Q3 FY27 report lands Tuesday, November 17. Nifty IT (−0.41% Thursday) trades this as a headline risk more than a fundamentals one — the payments-relevant read-across is enterprise AI/IT spend caution into the print.[^8][^9]
- **UPI MDR — day four with no date:** NPCI still has not notified the go-live for the 0.3–0.4% P2M levy above ₹2,000 (capped ₹300), four days past Deepavali and despite its promise to announce "immediately" after the festival; its spokesperson says "there is no announcement on this at the moment," while merchant bodies and fintechs lobby for a further delay. The Supreme Court challenge remains pending with no stay. ~96% of P2M volume stays exempt; zero-MDR rules apply until notified.[^10]
- **Who wins when it lands — the estimates keep stacking:** Paytm's UPI-MDR revenue is now estimated as high as **₹1,160 crore by FY28**, atop Bernstein's Outperform call (₹2,200 target, 24% FY26–30 revenue CAGR, "clear right to win" in merchant lending). Pine Labs management guides to ₹80–230 crore a year. PAYTM closed at its 52-week high Thursday (+7.31%); PINELABS added 5.82%.[^11]
- **Paytm's other tailwinds:** MSCI's November review raised Paytm's index weight (with Groww, Lenskart, Coal India among 11) effective close of November 30; the stock is +49% YTD and +20% off its October 26 low of ₹1,582.95. The unnotified MDR date remains the one binary overhang.[^11][^12]
- **IRDAI's EoM cap fight — PB Fintech defends its moat:** IRDAI proposes capping insurer expenses of management at 12.5% of premiums (life) and 20% of GDPI (general) over five years; Policybazaar's joint group CEO argued insurers would only spend 1–3% more on distribution beyond what Policybazaar already delivers, and that the platform improves claims ratios. Insurers' strong October premium data drove Thursday's insurance rally that POLICYBZR (+7.59%) rode. Regulation risk on the distribution economics is the story to watch.[^4][^13]
- **IPO pipeline watch:** Razorpay is being talked up for a 2027 listing — FY25 net loss of ₹1,209 crore (ESOP + redomiciling taxes), ~40% share of the SME payments segment, $180B TPV scale in the pitch books. Read-across for PINELABS/CCAVENUE valuations when the DRHP lands.[^14]
