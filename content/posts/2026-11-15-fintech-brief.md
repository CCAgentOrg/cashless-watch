# Fintech Brief — November 15, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### The UPI MDR decision lands today — and NPCI has already decided where part of the money goes

The 0.4% merchant fee on UPI payments above ₹2,000 was deferred past Diwali with no replacement date, and [sources tell Zee Business a decision is likely by November 15 — today](https://www.zeebiz.com/india/news-upi-mdr-decision-on-charges-for-payments-above-rs-2000-likely-by-november-15-say-sources-405479). Whatever the date, NPCI has already planned the spend: a [November 11 circular](https://indianstartupnews.com/news/mdr-on-upi-is-still-on-hold-but-npci-has-already-decided-how-part-of-it-will-be-spent-12806682) sets up the **UPI Vyapaar Saksham Scheme**, a three-year programme that pays acquiring banks ₹300 for every new physical QR code and ₹750 for every soundbox deployed at small merchants with turnover up to ₹40 lakh, plus volume-linked incentives (0.03% of eligible transaction value, 50% extra in targeted regions, 0.2% on incremental growth). The scheme only switches on when the MDR framework takes effect — effectively an admission that expanding acceptance infrastructure needs the merchant-fee money it ends nearly seven years of doing without. The wider fight continues in the Supreme Court, where [NPCI's November 3 counter affidavit says ~96% of merchant payments stay unaffected](https://indianstartupnews.com/news/mdr-on-upi-is-still-on-hold-but-npci-has-already-decided-how-part-of-it-will-be-spent-12806682), and in the numbers: the Finance Ministry pegs industry operating costs at ₹20,700 crore against a ₹2,000 crore budget allocation — under 10% covered.

Consumer takeaway: your payments under ₹2,000 and P2P transfers stay free, and small merchants remain exempt — but if the fee lands today, watch that no shop "adds MDR" to your bill. Pass-through to customers is not permitted.

### UPI just closed its biggest month ever, right before the fee debate

The scale behind that fight: [UPI processed a record 25.47 billion transactions worth ₹31.53 lakh crore in October](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-upi-p2m-p2p-transactions-october-2026) — the first month past ₹30 lakh crore, with average daily value crossing ₹1 lakh crore for the first time. Volume rose 5.8% over September's 24.07 billion and value 7.3% over ₹29.37 lakh crore; year-on-year, volume is up 23% and value 15.6%. The daily average hit roughly 822 million transactions across 758 live banks, with an average ticket of ₹1,238. The split matters for the MDR debate: merchant payments were 63.2% of volume (16.09 billion) but just 30.8% of value (₹9.71 lakh crore) — meaning most merchant activity sits precisely in the low-ticket band the fee framework leaves alone.

Consumer takeaway: the "small payments" UPI most of us actually use is statistically dominant, which is the strongest argument in the room for keeping the sub-₹2,000 band permanently free.

### Razorpay clears SEBI: the ₹5,000-6,000 crore IPO is on, listing eyed for Q1 2027

SEBI has issued its [final observations on Razorpay's confidentially-filed draft papers](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/technology/razorpay-moves-closer-to-public-market-debut-as-sebi-gives-green-signal-to-ipo/articleshow/135809192.cms) — dated November 3, disclosed in [the regulator's weekly update on November 13](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/ipo/sembcorp-green-infra-razorpay-makemytrip-get-sebi-nod-for-ipos-14079725.html) — clearing India's largest online payments gateway for an [IPO pegged at ₹5,000-6,000 crore targeting roughly a $5 billion valuation](https://www.bwdisrupt.com/article/razorpay-gets-sebi-nod-for-rs-5-000-6-000-cr-ipo-listing-likely-in-q1-2027-633553). Shareholders approved a fresh issue of up to ₹2,700 crore in May, alongside an offer for sale by existing investors; Axis Capital, Kotak Mahindra Capital, JPMorgan and Citi are the bankers. The confidential-route filing went in last June, and the company must now publish an updated draft for 21 days of public comment before the red herring prospectus — with a listing most likely between January and March 2027 and 18 months of runway from SEBI's observations.

Consumer takeaway: the merchant side of every UPI QR you scan is about to face quarterly public disclosure. An IPO doesn't change your checkout flow, but it does put Razorpay's fee economics and outage record on the record — material worth reading before you sign any merchant agreement, and useful pressure for pricing transparency across the gateways you use.

### Funding wrap: BFSI voice AI leads a thin week; A2B's $180 million deal towers over it

It was a quiet week for pure fintech funding. The standout for the sector: Bengaluru's Desible.ai, which builds [voice-AI agents for bank, NBFC and insurer workflows, raised ₹32 crore in a seed-plus round led by Prime Venture Partners](https://indianstartupnews.com/funding/voice-ai-is-our-entry-point-desibleai-raises-rs-32-crore-to-automate-bfsi-workflows-12804358), with existing backer Invention Engine participating. Across the broader market, [Entrackr's weekly tally counted 12 startups raising $31.3 million](https://entrackr.com/report/funding-and-acquisitions-in-indian-startups-this-week-nov-09-nov-14-12808553) (two growth-stage, ten early-stage, five undisclosed) — dwarfed by the headline non-venture deal: Bodhi Tree Systems taking a majority stake in Chennai's Adyar Ananda Bhavan restaurant chain for $180 million. No large rounds in payments or consumer credit landed in the last 24 hours.

Consumer takeaway: VC money is flowing into AI that answers your bank's compliance calls before it flows into products that cut your fees. When a fintech's customer is the bank, your grievances ride on whatever the bank buys — worth remembering the next time an IVR "agent" handles your fraud complaint.
