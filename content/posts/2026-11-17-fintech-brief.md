# Fintech Brief — November 17, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### NPCI wants Google to harden Android against device-level UPI fraud

India's payments industry has taken an unusual step: [asking Google to fix Android itself](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/fight-against-cyber-frauds-npci-upi-apps-tap-google-to-fix-android-chinks/articleshow/135900825.cms). The [National Payments Corporation of India has begun discussions with Google](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/indias-payments-industry-asks-google-to-secure-android-payments/tldr) on introducing operating-system-level safeguards against UPI frauds that exploit vulnerabilities in the phone rather than mistakes by the user. For years, fraud prevention assumed a careless customer — an OTP shared, a fake QR scanned, a deceptive collect request approved. Attackers have moved on: they now exploit legitimate OS features and sideloaded apps. Industry officials want Android to [restrict critical permissions for sideloaded APKs and strictly block apps installed outside vetted stores from requesting accessibility services, device admin or overlay permissions without multi-step safeguards](https://urbanacres.in/upi-fraud-threat-pushes-npci-to-seek-stronger-android-safeguards). The push is also self-interested: payment apps absorb substantial fraud liabilities for attacks that are outside their power to stop.

Consumer takeaway: the most dangerous UPI fraud today may not need your password at all — just a malicious app you installed from outside the Play Store. Stick to official app stores, and treat any app demanding "accessibility" permissions with suspicion, however legitimate it looks.

### Wholesale inflation at 12.29%, retail above 6%: more rate hikes are coming, and EMIs will feel it

The inflation squeeze tightened on both fronts. [India's wholesale price index jumped 12.29% in October](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/india-wholesale-prices-rise-1229-year-on-year-october-2026-11-16), driven by fuel and power costs from the prolonged West Asia crisis, while retail inflation had already [breached the RBI's 6% upper tolerance band at 6.09% — the first breach in two years](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/economists-see-50-75-bps-more-rbi-rate-hikes-terminal-rate-at-6-6-25-126111601589_1.html). Economists now expect the RBI to [deliver another 50-75 basis points of hikes, taking the repo rate to 6-6.25%](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/economists-see-50-75-bps-more-rbi-rate-hikes-terminal-rate-at-6-6-25-126111601589_1.html) from 5.5% after October's first hike in nearly four years. ICRA's Aditi Nayar calls a December hike "almost certain"; the MPC meets December 2-4. Brickwork Ratings expects [at least one more 25 bps hike before a pause](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/business/wholesale-price-inflation-surges-to-1229-in-october-4220305). The direct consumer consequence: [home loan EMIs are set to rise](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/personal-finance/rising-inflation-may-push-up-home-loan-emis-here-s-how-much-more-could-borrowers-pay-if-rbi-hikes-repo-rate-14081192.html), with floating-rate borrowers bearing the brunt.

Consumer takeaway: if you're on a floating-rate home or personal loan, budget for higher EMIs through the December policy and beyond. Fixed-rate refinance windows are closing — worth a comparison before the next MPC announcement.

### SEBI opens up the IPO rulebook — and may relax scrutiny of foreign fund ownership

SEBI has [constituted a 10-member sub-group of its Primary Market Advisory Committee, headed by NSE MD & CEO Ashish Chauhan](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/indias-markets-regulator-forms-panel-simplify-capital-raising-rules-2026-11-16), to review key provisions of the Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements (ICDR) Regulations, 2018 — the rulebook for [IPOs, FPOs, preferential issues and qualified institutional placements](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/news/sebi-sets-up-panel-to-review-key-icdr-provisions-covering-ipos-fpos-qips/articleshow/135893002.cms). The terms of reference cover eligibility conditions for issuers across fundraising modes and [lock-in requirements on promoters' holdings, pre-issue capital and preferential allotments](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/markets/lock-ins-to-price-bands-sebi-sets-up-panel-to-review-ipo-qip-preferential-issue-rules-12334259) — with the stated goal of easier market access while preserving disclosure and investor protection. In parallel, the regulator is reportedly [planning to raise the FPI beneficial-ownership disclosure threshold from 10% to 25%](https://money.rediff.com/news/market/sebi-to-propose-raising-fpi-beneficial-ownership-to-25/58161220261116), easing compliance for foreign funds to align with global standards.

Consumer takeaway: lock-in rules are what stop promoters from dumping shares the day a listing pops. If they're relaxed, retail investors in fresh IPOs carry more of the early-exit risk — watch this review's consultation paper before crediting the next "simplified" IPO regime.

### On RuPay credit cards linked to UPI, the two biggest apps are trailing

[NPCI's own data shows PhonePe and Google Pay — the two largest UPI apps — offering fewer RuPay credit card on UPI features than smaller rivals](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/phonepe-google-pay-trail-smaller-apps-in-rupay-credit-card-upi-features-126111601733_1.html). Since the RBI allowed RuPay credit cards to be linked to UPI in 2022, the facility has remained exclusive to the RuPay and UPI networks, both run by NPCI. Industry sources believe the larger players simply aren't competing on this feature, even as NPCI pushes adoption. That matters because credit-on-UPI is the main way small merchants can accept card payments without a POS machine — and the merchant's acquiring cost and the customer's credit experience both depend on which app and card they use.

Consumer takeaway: if you want to spend credit over UPI QR codes, your best feature set today may sit in a smaller app — compare RuPay credit card on UPI support before defaulting to the app everyone else uses.

It was a thin Monday for Indian fintech funding — the Olyv round we covered yesterday was the main event. The largest India tech raise of the day went instead to [semiconductor startup Bharat Semi, which raised $24 million (~₹215 crore) in seed funding](https://www.indiatoday.in/technology/news/story/indian-startup-bharat-semi-bags-rs-215-crore-will-now-develop-next-gen-ai-chips-for-global-markets-3039818-2026-11-16) led by [Info Edge Group, South Park Commons and Indus Bridge Ventures](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news/delhis-bharat-semi-raises-24-mn-to-develop-ai-image-sensors) to build AI-enabled image sensors with design wins from General Atomics and Kaynes Semicon. Separately, at the Egypt-India Startup Forum, [commerce minister Piyush Goyal urged fintechs of both countries to co-create cross-border payment systems](https://government.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/digital-payments/goyal-urges-india-egypt-fintechs-to-co-create-co-build-cross-border-payment-systems/135880884), positioning India's UPI journey alongside Egypt's InstaPay.
