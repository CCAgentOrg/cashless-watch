## 📰 Sector News

- **Paytm's wallet gets its RBI moment — and the stock still fell:** The RBI granted **in-principle approval to Paytm Payments Services Ltd (PPSL) to operate prepaid payment instruments, including wallets** — reversing the January 2024 crackdown that killed PPBL's wallet business. Jefferies calls it an "avenue for monetisation," raised its target to **₹2,300 from ₹2,150 (Buy)** and noted the target now sits above the IPO price for the first time in this rally. Context: wallets are ~1% of digital payments, but Paytm held **>60% of wallet transaction value** until Dec 2023. Yet PAYTM fell 2.53% Monday — three-session profit-taking after touching ₹1,960 (52-wk high) — classic "good news in the price" behaviour. The unnotified UPI-MDR date remains the binary overhang.[^5][^6][^7]
- **Bank Nifty's record is 189 points away — the fifth session of foreign buying decides it:** FIIs bought an 11th straight session Monday and FPIs have put ₹89,856 crore into equities in the first half of November; Bank Nifty's intraday high of 61,712.80 missed the record by 52 points. Kotak is setting fresh highs daily, HDFC Bank is the volume laggard still 19.8% below its peak, and ICICI rests 1.9% from its own. A flat GIFT Nifty open means the record print likely needs either a strong Nvidia-aftermath bounce or another crude-down session (supply-glut forecasts: Goldman sees Brent averaging ~$88 in 2027, toward $78 by year-end).[^1][^8]
- **UPI MDR — day six with no date, but a merchant-incentive scheme appeared:** NPCI has still not notified the go-live for the 0.3–0.4% P2M levy above ₹2,000 (capped ₹300) despite Zee Business reporting a decision was expected "by November 15"; zero-MDR rules remain in force. What did land is NPCI's **Nov 11 "UPI Vyapaar Saksham" scheme — ₹300 per QR code and ₹750 per soundbox for merchants, running three years from MDR go-live** — the onboarding groundwork ahead of the levy. ~96% of P2M volume stays exempt; the Supreme Court challenge remains pending without a stay. Watch for a notification this week — Paytm/Pine Labs estimates (₹1,160 cr and ₹80–230 cr annual revenue respectively) hinge on it.[^9][^10][^11]
- **Nvidia's first post-Huang print lands tonight — the IT index traded the anticipation flat:** NVDA fell 4.1% Monday ahead of Q3 FY27 results (after the US bell tonight), with the FT reporting Huang and allies are plotting a comeback while the board says reinstatement isn't even on the agenda (an investigation is). Nasdaq is in correction (>10% below its Oct 6 record), SOX fell 5.3%, and the Trump administration added a new rule restricting China's access to advanced AI chips ahead of a Trump-Xi meeting. Nifty IT's flat close (+0.03% after a 715-point intraday swing) says India priced the fear early; tonight's print sets Wednesday's tone for IT, and the read-across for payments-tech (INFIBEAM/NPST enterprise spend) is caution into the release.[^2][^3][^12]
- **NPST's earnings-call week:** Q2 FY27 consolidated PAT rose 34% YoY to ₹13.22 cr with income up 50% to ₹72.95 cr; management reaffirmed **60–70% FY27 revenue growth** on H2 execution; a $1M OCD investment in its wholly-owned UAE subsidiary (4% coupon) filed Nov 15. The stock spiked to ₹1,855 before fading to +0.85% — the market wants H2 numbers, not guidance. Its Q2 call audio is public on npstx.com.[^13][^14][^15]
