# Fintech Brief — November 18, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### UPI MDR: the industry wants a date, merchants still pay zero

The Payments Council of India has [made multiple representations to the finance ministry and NPCI](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/banking/payment-firms-seek-clarity-on-mdr-rollout-for-select-upi-transactions/articleshow/135939899.cms) seeking an early announcement of the final implementation date for the merchant discount rate on select UPI transactions — a week past Diwali, the levy that was deferred "immediately post Diwali" on October 13 [still has no notified date](https://indianstartupnews.com/news/mdr-on-upi-is-still-on-hold-but-npci-has-already-decided-how-part-of-it-will-be-spent-12806682). The framework itself is settled: [0.4% MDR on person-to-merchant UPI payments above ₹2,000, capped at ₹300 for transactions of ₹75,000 and above](https://www.livemint.com/market/stock-market-news/upi-mdr-rollout-after-diwali-what-it-means-for-paytm-mobikwik-and-pine-labs-what-investors-should-know-11794477959612.html), leaving roughly 96% of P2M volume exempt. NPCI chief executive Dilip Asbe has [estimated the ecosystem's annual operating cost at around ₹21,000 crore](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/banking/payment-firms-seek-clarity-on-mdr-rollout-for-select-upi-transactions/articleshow/135939899.cms), with the levy expected to recover ₹13,000–15,000 crore in year one. NPCI's November 11 [Vyapaar Saksham scheme](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-npci-upi-mdr-vyapaar-saksham-scheme) — ₹300 per QR code and ₹750 per soundbox, funded from the MDR pool — is already banking on a go-live. Until the notification lands, [zero-MDR rules remain in force](https://indianstartupnews.com/news/mdr-on-upi-is-still-on-hold-but-npci-has-already-decided-how-part-of-it-will-be-spent-12806682) and the Supreme Court challenge proceeds without a stay.

Consumer takeaway: nothing changes for you today — no merchant pays MDR yet, and small-ticket payments may never carry it. When the date is notified, watch whether merchants above ₹2,000 quietly build the fee into prices.

### UPI's record October: 25.47 billion transactions, ₹31.53 lakh crore

[NPCI's October numbers show UPI crossing ₹31.53 lakh crore in monthly value for the first time](https://www.jagranjosh.com/general-knowledge/upi-creates-history-in-october-2026-transactions-cross-3153-lakh-crore-for-the-first-time-1820013888-1), on 25.47 billion transactions — volume up 23% and value up 16% year-on-year, powered by festive shopping and online sales. Two firsts stand out: average daily transaction value [crossed ₹1 lakh crore for the first time](https://www.jagranjosh.com/general-knowledge/upi-creates-history-in-october-2026-transactions-cross-3153-lakh-crore-for-the-first-time-1820013888-1), and the momentum carried into November, with [the first four days averaging ₹1.29 lakh crore a day](https://www.facebook.com/deepak86dxb/posts/1141317911643773). The scale matters for the MDR debate above: even a sliver of a 25-billion-transaction month sitting above the ₹2,000 threshold is a meaningful revenue line for payment apps — and a meaningful cost line for merchants.

Consumer takeaway: record volume cuts both ways — UPI's reach is why even a "small" 0.4% fee on big-ticket merchant payments moves lakhs of crores. Keep small-merchant QR payments under ₹2,000 in mind when the fee goes live.

### RBI's six-hour rule meets a live target: payment aggregators in hackers' crosshairs

Expert commentary on the RBI's [Master Directions on Cyber Resilience for non-bank payment system operators](https://cfo.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/governance-risk-compliance/rbis-6-hour-reporting-rule-to-help-curb-cyber-threats-fraud-in-digital-payments/135908582) — which require NPCI, card networks, payment aggregators, PPI issuers and white-label ATM operators to report cyberattacks, critical outages and settlement delays to the RBI and CERT-In [within six hours of detection](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/banking/rbis-6-hour-reporting-rule-to-help-curb-cyber-threats-say-experts/articleshow/135864134.cms) — landed the same week reporting that [attackers who exploited NBFC payout APIs have moved up the chain to payment aggregators](https://theheadandtale.com/deepdives/ai-hackers-move-up-the-chain-payment-aggregators-now-in-the-crosshairs-after-nbfc-attacks), with firms including PayU and Open among the targeted. The direction's six-hour window exists precisely for this: a compromised aggregator sits between thousands of merchants and the banking network, so containment speed is systemic. Separately, [RBI and Yes Bank have filed review petitions in the Supreme Court on the ₹8,415 crore AT1 bond write-off verdict](https://www.sahi.com/news/yes-bank-faces-rbi-sc-review-petition-over-8-415-crore-bond-write-off), while Yes Bank [refuted reports of a $3.4 million cyber fraud, confirming zero financial loss](https://www.sahi.com/news/yes-bank-faces-rbi-sc-review-petition-over-8-415-crore-bond-write-off).

Consumer takeaway: faster regulator notification means faster takedowns of mule accounts and faster public warnings. If your bank or payment app suffers an outage, the six-hour rule is why the RBI should already know before you tweet at them.

### SEBI rewrites commodity margin rules from December 1

SEBI has [revised the minimum initial margin and minimum Margin Period of Risk requirements for the commodity derivatives segment](https://legal.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/regulators/sebi-revises-minimum-margin-norms-for-commodity-derivatives/135934389) via a circular issued November 17, [effective December 1](https://www.bignewsnetwork.com/news/279438820/sebi-revises-minimum-margin-requirements-for-commodity-derivatives-new-norms-effective-december-1) and applicable for one year, extendable. Market infrastructure institutions must set up systems, amend bye-laws and inform participants. Margin Period of Risk is the cushion brokers hold against the worst plausible day — raising the minimums means commodity traders (gold, silver, crude, agri contracts) will need to post somewhat more collateral per position, and brokers will pass that through as higher margins. The regulator has separately [constituted a sub-group of its Primary Market Advisory Committee to review the ICDR Regulations](https://www.caclubindia.com/notice_circulars/sebi-constitutes-pmac-sub-group-to-review-and-rationalise-icdr-regulations-2018-11681.asp) — the IPO rulebook — continuing this week's pattern of SEBI both tightening risk buffers and loosening market access.

Consumer takeaway: if you trade commodity derivatives, expect margin requirements to step up from December 1 — budget for more locked-in collateral, and check your broker's circular before the change hits your ledger.

### Funding note: the day's fintech money went to AI that answers the phone

The freshest fintech-adjacent raise of the day went to [Karta AI, which picked up ₹28.52 crore (~$3 million) in a seed round led by Peer Capital and Cedar Hill Capital](https://india.entrepreneur.com/business-news/karta-ai-raises-inr-28-52-crore-seed-round), with Wyser VC, CDM Capital and Seedstars participating. Founded in 2024 and operated as Dreamzest Technologies, the Mumbai-based startup builds [AI voice agents that handle inbound customer calls for banks, lenders, fintechs and insurers](https://www.bwdisrupt.com/article/karta-ai-raises-rs-28-52-cr-in-seed-round-led-by-peer-capital-cedar-hill-capital-634028) — capital earmarked for distribution, hiring and its predictive context engine. Watch this space with a skeptic's eye: banks outsourcing first-contact resolution to voice AI raises the stakes on fraud controls, since voice channels are already the weakest link in social-engineering attacks.
