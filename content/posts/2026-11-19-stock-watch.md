## 📰 Sector News

- **Nvidia passed the earnings test and the market flunked it anyway:** the chip giant's Q3 beat ($115.8B revenue, raised FY guidance to ~75% growth) was followed by a **−2.9% Wednesday session** — the stock has lost over a third of its value since early October and ~$750 billion of market value since Jensen Huang's November 12 resignation, with the Nasdaq ~12% off its October record (correction territory). CoreWeave, Qualcomm and Cerebras fell alongside.[^4][^5] Overlaying it all: **Fed Governor Waller told CNBC the Fed needs a 50-basis-point December hike or a faster 2027 pace, the dollar printed a fresh 19-month high, and hawkish October FOMC minutes echoed the same** — the rate story, not the earnings story, is driving risk.[^1][^4] Read-across for the payments-tech tier (CCAVENUE, NPST enterprise spend) stays cautious until the AI trade finds a floor.
- **Anthropic's record IPO debuts today:** the Claude lab priced the **largest IPO of all time, raising $81 billion** despite a $104 billion loss — the next big sentiment test for the AI trade after Nvidia's sell-the-news session.[^13][^4] Nifty IT's bounce-or-break is the domestic echo.
- **UPI MDR — day nine, still no date, but the shape is getting clearer:** the Payments Council of India has made **multiple representations to the finance ministry and NPCI seeking an early, firm implementation date** after the post-Diwali deferral — under the September 15 framework, a 40-basis-point MDR applies to eligible bank-to-merchant UPI transactions above ₹2,000, with small transactions and exempt categories staying free.[^8] Separately, an **NPCI panel has settled on a flat ₹5 fee for merchant-to-merchant (B2B) UPI transactions** — dramatically below the ₹80–300 charges traders feared under the standard 0.4% rate (₹75,000 payment would have cost ₹300), though the go-live date remains unfinalised.[^9] The MDR-funded **UPI Vyapaar Saksham scheme** (November 11 circular; ₹300/QR, ₹750/soundbox, transaction-linked incentives; runs three years from MDR go-live) is ready and waiting for that date.[^10] Every Paytm/Pine Labs MDR-revenue estimate stays frozen until it drops.
- **PB Fintech's cofounder sets his exit:** Alok Bansal — executive vice-chairman and wholetime director — relinquishes his executive role **effective March 31, 2027**. POLICYBZR enters the session already testing its ₹1,025 floor; watch for broker commentary on succession and the insurance-agency engine's continuity.[^11]
- **SBI's November run has a fundamental tail:** the stock is up ~16% this month (vs ~10% for the Sensex) on brokerages' constructive read — credit growth, credit costs and capital as the pillars of RoA/RoE predictability — and the wider bank rally (Nifty Bank +13% in November, PNB +23%/SBI +16.5% over a month) rests on strong credit growth, stable asset quality and easing funding pressures.[^6][^7]
