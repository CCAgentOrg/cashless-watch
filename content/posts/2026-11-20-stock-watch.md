## 📰 Sector News

- **The MDR-delay crash, decoded:** the Financial Express reported that the UPI MDR rollout for eligible P2M transactions above ₹2,000 **could be deferred further — with one industry source calling a push past the 2027 Uttar Pradesh elections a worst-case scenario** — even though NPCI's UPI and Services Steering Committee has settled the structure (40 bps on eligible bank-to-merchant transactions; the flat ₹5 B2B fee) and most operational groundwork is done. The government, RBI and NPCI have simply not agreed on a date.[^12][^13] Merchant-side resistance is growing: the All India Consumer Products Distributors Federation wants M2M transactions exempted entirely.[^13] Meanwhile the Payments Council of India has made **multiple representations to the finance ministry and NPCI** for a firm timeline — and flagged that government incentives for processing UPI and RuPay debit transactions have been **outstanding for over 18 months** while technology, acquisition and compliance costs keep rising.[^14] Reuters confirms the final date decision, to be made in consultation with the RBI and government, is yet to be taken; NPCI and the finance ministry did not respond to queries.[^15] Sell-side defended: Bernstein kept its Paytm Buy (PT ₹2,200) calling the MDR delay "the most immediate risk" to near-term earnings but the risk-reward attractive, and Jefferies is at Buy/₹2,300.[^5][^10] The other overhang on Paytm: potential merchant-lending regulation (IRR caps on digital lending) that could hit profitability independent of MDR.[^4]
- **Wall Street's rout is Friday's problem:** the Dow fell 1,101 points (−2.19%) to 49,097, the S&P 500 dropped 2.00% for its sixth straight loss (down 8.2% from September's record) and the Nasdaq sank 2.2% — **more than 90% of S&P constituents fell** as the AI unwind continued despite Anthropic's debut.[^8][^9] The record IPO couldn't hold the tape: Anthropic priced at $83, opened at $111.50 and closed +17% at $97.17, but faded hard intraday while AMD sank 7.7% on a Hunterbrook short report alleging ties to blacklisted Chinese server makers (AMD denies) and Walmart fell on a soft full-year outlook.[^9] Oil rose (Brent ~$92–93) and gold fell ~1.8% — hawkish Fed minutes (almost all officials see more hikes) still own the rate story. Nifty IT's −1.10% Thursday session was the domestic echo; a ~150-point GIFT Nifty discount extends it into Friday's open.[^7]
- **Bank Nifty's record close has the right fuel — for now:** the first-ever 63,000 print rode record November FPI inflows (over ₹1 lakh crore for the month so far; FIIs bought ₹5,866.78 crore on Wednesday alone while DIIs sold ₹3,159.90 crore), falling crude/copper easing the inflation-and-external-balance picture, and constructive brokerage reads on credit growth and asset quality.[^1][^3][^16] All 12 constituents closed green — the question for Friday is whether a 2% Wall Street session is enough to break a streak that strong.
- **Paytm's structural put:** even after Thursday's crash, the stock is ~85% above its March 52-week low and the RBI's preliminary approval for Paytm Payments Services to issue prepaid payment products (wallet business run independently post-PPB wind-down) remains the structural backstop investors cite while waiting for the MDR date.[^11]
- **Calendar:** the UPI MDR date notification is *the* event (PCI representations pending; NPCI committee structure settled, date unfinalised); **RBI policy December 4** is the macro marker; watch for broker-note follow-through on Paytm/Pine Labs after the crash, and the AI-trade read-through (Nifty IT) after Wall Street's sixth straight loss. UPI's October record month (25.47 billion txns, ₹31.53 lakh crore) keeps the ecosystem-data backdrop strong even as the fee story stalls.
