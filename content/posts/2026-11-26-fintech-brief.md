# Fintech Brief — November 26, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### PhonePe points its IPO at March 2027 — and its prospectus at two pending UPI rules

PhonePe has restarted talks with investment bankers for a March 2027 listing, targeting a $12–15 billion valuation with an offer for sale of up to ₹12,000 crore, and plans to file updated IPO papers by December-end ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/phonepe-revives-ipo-plans-eyes-march-2027-listing-at-up-to-15-bn-valuation)). The company paused the process in March citing geopolitical uncertainty and market volatility; the rally in Indian equities and foreign investors returning to net buying appear to have changed its mind. The pitch rests on numbers: lending business growth of around 100%, operating revenue up 35–40% quarter-on-quarter, and an expected net profit — a turnaround from [FY26's ₹2,792 crore loss](https://inc42.com/buzz/ipo-bound-phonepe-posts-%E2%82%B92792-cr-loss-in-fy26-revenue-up-11-5-yoy/) on ₹7,920.5 crore revenue ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/phonepe-revives-ipo-plans-eyes-march-2027-listing-at-up-to-15-bn-valuation)).

But PhonePe's own filing timeline is hostage to two regulatory questions it wants clarity on by December-end ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/phonepe-revives-ipo-plans-eyes-march-2027-listing-at-up-to-15-bn-valuation)): whether the UPI merchant fee (MDR) framework actually lands, and what happens to NPCI's 30% market-share cap for third-party UPI apps — a cap PhonePe operates above, with the compliance deadline currently set for December 31, 2026. It may not be first in the queue anyway: Razorpay already holds [SEBI's approval for a ₹5,000–6,000 crore IPO](https://inc42.com/buzz/razorpay-gets-sebi-nod-for-%E2%82%B96000-cr-ipo/) targeting a January–March 2027 debut.

Consumer takeaway: an OFS-heavy listing is existing investors selling shares to the public, not money flowing into the business. What you should watch is the regulatory clarity — because MDR and the market-share cap will shape what payments cost and how concentrated your payment apps stay.

### UPI's merchant fee may now wait until after the 2027 elections

The MDR rollout is slipping again. Reports on November 25–26 indicate the government may hold off implementing the merchant discount rate on UPI until after the 2027 assembly elections in Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, Uttarakhand, Goa and Manipur, wary of merchant and trader pushback in an election year ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/upi-mdr-rollout-may-be-delayed-until-after-2027-assembly-elections-report-14084125.html); [Business Today via Ground News](https://ground.news/article/central-government-preparing-to-take-big-decision-on-upi-direct-charging-till-2027)). The framework announced in September proposes 0.4% MDR on merchant payments above ₹2,000, capped at ₹300 per transaction for payments of ₹75,000 and above — person-to-person transfers and merchant payments up to ₹2,000 stay free, as do small merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh a month via QR codes ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/upi-mdr-rollout-may-be-delayed-until-after-2027-assembly-elections-report-14084125.html)). Estimates peg first-year collections at ₹13,000–15,000 crore ([Indian Startup News, citing Financial Express](https://www.instagram.com/p/Df6jPNeliDN)).

The resistance is organised. Traders' bodies floated a "No UPI Day" protest in October before withdrawing it after meeting Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/upi-mdr-rollout-may-be-delayed-until-after-2027-assembly-elections-report-14084125.html)); distributors are now lobbying for exemptions on merchant-to-merchant transfers ([Business Today via Ground News](https://ground.news/article/central-government-preparing-to-take-big-decision-on-upi-direct-charging-till-2027)). NPCI had already deferred the original October 15 start date citing the festive season. Regulatory and operational preparations are reportedly complete — this is now purely a political timing call ([Indian Startup News, citing Financial Express](https://www.instagram.com/p/Df6jPNeliDN)).

Consumer takeaway: nothing changes for your UPI payments yet, and the fee — whenever it comes — is legally the merchant's burden. The real risk is indirect: merchants padding prices or nudging customers back to cash for big bills. Watch whether the government keeps enforcing its own stance that MDR must not be passed to customers.

### NPCI's new dispute rules went live yesterday — what changes for you

Two NPCI circulars are now in force. The first ([OC-240](https://www.npci.org.in/uploads/UPI_URCS_OC_240_Standardization_in_Dispute_Management_6f4a6542c3.pdf), effective November 25) standardises the categories banks use to classify disputes — including excess charges and duplicate payments — and lists what evidence supports an excess-charge case: the customer complaint, the UPI payment receipt and the purchase bill or invoice ([Gadgets Now](https://gadgetsnow.indiatimes.com/tech-news/how-indian-shoppers-can-dispute-excess-charges-on-upi-payments/articleshow/136150234.cms)). The second ([OC-241](https://www.npci.org.in/uploads/UPI_URCS_OC_241_Revision_of_dispute_routing_logic_for_UPI_P2_M_transactions_in_URCS_1c6c076393.pdf), from December 23) reroutes many merchant-payment disputes to the bank that onboarded the merchant, rather than the bank holding the merchant's settlement account.

For consumers, the practical shifts are modest but real. You still start a complaint the same way — the transaction in your app, the merchant, or your bank — and you keep paying nothing for it. But if you're disputing an excess charge, the circular just told your bank exactly which three documents to expect, and from December 23 the bank that signed the shop up for UPI — the one that can actually check its merchant records — becomes answerable for many merchant-side disputes. RBI's failed-transaction protections are untouched: auto-reversal within T+5 calendar days plus ₹100 per day of delay still applies for the specified failure cases ([Gadgets Now](https://gadgetsnow.indiatimes.com/tech-news/how-indian-shoppers-can-dispute-excess-charges-on-upi-payments/articleshow/136150234.cms)).

Consumer takeaway: neither circular creates an automatic refund for every completed payment. Keep the invoice and the UPI receipt together — the gap between them is your case.

### SEBI cuts the KYC repeat-tax: one submission, many intermediaries

SEBI issued a circular on November 25 simplifying client onboarding and rationalising the risk-management framework at KYC Registration Agencies (KRAs), effective December 24, 2026 ([SEBI circular HO/38/14/(25), via LiveLaw](https://www.livelawbiz.com/securities-law/sebi/securities-and-exchange-board-of-india-simplifies-kyc-framework-eases-client-onboarding-documentation-requirements-559984); [CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/sebi-simplifies-kyc-process-for-investors-6-major-changes-effective-december-24-20041399.htm)). The headline changes: supplementary client information from the account-opening form gets centralised at the KRA level so investors stop re-submitting it to every broker, mutual fund and depository; KRA records verified against official databases become "validated" and portable across intermediaries; mobile verification is relaxed where the number is Aadhaar-seeded and already verified; and documentation eases for name changes and overseas address proof for certain residents ([CNBC TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/sebi-simplifies-kyc-process-for-investors-6-major-changes-effective-december-24-20041399.htm); [The Hindu BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/sebi-simplifies-kyc-norms-streamlines-investor-onboarding/article71743649.ece)). KRAs must publish an implementation SOP within 30 days.

Consumer takeaway: this attacks a real cost — duplicated KYC submissions, lapsed re-verifications and onboarding friction that pushes people toward informal channels. The test is December 24: whether opening your second demat account actually takes minutes, not another form pile.
