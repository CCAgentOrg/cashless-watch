# Fintech Brief — November 27, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### RBI's stop switch: a kill-cord for your bank account, with a catch

RBI has released draft directions under its Responsible Business Conduct framework that give account holders two new fraud controls: a **stop switch** and a **Trusted Person Mechanism**. The drafts went out on November 23 with comments open until **December 14, 2026**; most safeguards kick in from **July 1, 2027**, and banks can move earlier ([TaxGuru, on the Fifth Amendment Directions draft](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-draft-directions-2026-propose-digital-payment-stop-switch-trusted-person-authentication.html); [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/rbi-proposes-new-rules-to-protect-bank-accounts-from-fraud-heres-how-stop-switch-and-trusted-person-approval-work-11795441662320.html)).

The stop switch lets you switch off digital payment channels on your own account — transaction-wise, beneficiary-wise and daily limits included — so a compromised account can be frozen by its owner, not by a helpline queue. Incoming credits keep flowing, and recurring mandates, standing instructions, lien-marked debits and bank charges continue despite the switch ([casamaaj, on the draft provisions](https://www.casamaaj.com/news/rbi-stop-switch-trusted-person-draft-directions); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/rbi-proposes-greater-customer-control-over-digital-payments-to-curb-fraud-126112301054_1.html)).

The Trusted Person Mechanism is the more novel bit: you designate a person (18+, with consent) and set a threshold. Any transfer above it pings that person with the amount, beneficiary name, account number and your narration — and they get **one hour** to approve. No response or a rejection means the transfer dies. The trusted person cannot see your balance or initiate payments, and changing them or the threshold requires authentication plus a 24-hour cooling period ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/rbi-proposes-greater-customer-control-over-digital-payments-to-curb-fraud-126112301054_1.html); [casamaaj](https://www.casamaaj.com/news/rbi-stop-switch-trusted-person-draft-directions)).

Here's the catch for a payments-heavy country: **UPI transfers linked to your account, merchant payments on any instrument, and recurring e-mandate payments are all excluded** from the trusted-person check ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/rbi-proposes-greater-customer-control-over-digital-payments-to-curb-fraud-126112301054_1.html)). The mechanisms target the classic impersonation scams — "your relative needs money urgently" — over net banking, IMPS and RTGS, not the everyday scan-and-pay flow. RBI has also broadened its own negligence test: a bank that fails to implement multi-factor authentication, skips mandatory transaction alerts, or doesn't run 24×7 fraud reporting can now be treated as negligent in unauthorised-transaction disputes ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-draft-directions-2026-propose-digital-payment-stop-switch-trusted-person-authentication.html)).

Consumer takeaway: this is worth commenting on by December 14. The one-hour approval window is tight for elderly account holders whose trusted person is at work; the UPI carve-out is defensible but leaves the largest scam surface untouched. The draft is directionally right — the burden of stopping fraud shifts a notch toward the bank and away from the victim.

### Finnable raises ₹405 crore as NBFC lending keeps its hot streak

Bengaluru-based digital lender Finnable has raised **₹405 crore in a Series C** led by Bharat Value Fund (₹150 crore), the Pantomath Group-linked fund, with new investors Pratithi Investments — the investment firm of Infosys co-founder Kris Gopalakrishnan — and NKSquared, the family arm of Zerodha's Nithin and Nikhil Kamath. TVS Capital and Claypond Capital, Ranjan Pai's family office, re-upped from the existing side ([BW Disrupt](https://www.bwdisrupt.com/article/nbfc-firm-finnable-raises-rs-405-cr-in-series-c-led-by-bharat-value-fund-635272); [Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/tech/funding/digital-lender-finnable-raises-rs-405-crore-to-expand-lending-portfolio/articleshow/136178433.cms)).

The numbers explain the interest: assets under management have grown from ₹1,015 crore in March 2023 to over ₹4,600 crore in October 2026 — a 52.5% CAGR — and H1 FY27 profit of ₹51 crore already beats FY26's full-year ₹46 crore ([BW Disrupt](https://www.bwdisrupt.com/article/nbfc-firm-finnable-raises-rs-405-cr-in-series-c-led-by-bharat-value-fund-635272); [Inc42](https://www.facebook.com/Inc42/posts/digital-lending-startup-finnable-raises-405-cr-in-series-c-round-the-funding-round-was-led-by-bh/1726832905707491)). Finnable lends ₹25,000–₹10 lakh personal loans and loans against property to salaried borrowers the banks won't underwrite, through co-lending partnerships. It comes a year after a ₹500 crore round led by Z47 and TVS Capital ([The Head and Tale](https://theheadandtale.com/fintech-news/finnable-raises-series-c-funding-from-bharat-value-fund-pratithi-investments-others)).

Consumer takeaway: profitable NBFCs mean more credit supply for thin-file borrowers — but this is also the segment where collections pressure and aggressive recovery practices concentrate. Cheaper capital should, in theory, mean cheaper loans; watch whether Finnable's rates actually fall.

### Vegapay's $10 million bet: credit on UPI rails, rented out to banks

Mumbai-based fintech infrastructure startup Vegapay raised **$10 million (about ₹93.5 crore) in Series A** funding co-led by Prosus and Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia & India, with Elevation Capital and Eximius Ventures participating — a sharp step up from its pre-Series A valuation just four months earlier ([Inc42](https://seedtable.com/companies/vegapay/funding-rounds/series-a-2026-11); [BEAMSTART](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DgMDy8psDwu)).

Vegapay builds the plumbing banks and NBFCs use to issue credit cards and run lending programmes — its platform powers credit lines on UPI and card programmes for Paytm, BharatPe, Kiwi, Scapia and EbixCash World Money, and manages roughly 20 lakh credit accounts, including work with BOBCARDS ([Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/companies/vegapay/funding-rounds/series-a-2026-11)). The fresh money goes into scaling its UPI credit and card platforms ([Inc42](https://seedtable.com/companies/vegapay/funding-rounds/series-a-2026-11)).

Consumer takeaway: credit-on-UPI is coming to more banks because vendors like Vegapay make it cheap to launch. A ₹25,000 credit line that appears in your UPI app in three taps is convenient — read the interest rate before you tap, because this credit is priced like unsecured personal lending, not like your scan-and-pay.
