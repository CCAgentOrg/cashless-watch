## 📰 Sector News

- **IT carried the tape; banks sat out.** Thursday's +3.66% Nifty IT surge — closed at its day high, HCL Tech/BSE/TCS leading — was the second day of the IT bid, and the rotation is now explicit: money chasing the anti-AI trade into Indian tech while the bank complex that made November digests Monday's record-wick crash.[^3][^4] The Nifty's failed probe of 26,000 (25,974.95 intraday high) plus Bank Nifty's −0.26% close says the index is waiting for leadership to hand over from IT back to financials — Friday's first hour tells you whether that happens or consolidation rules below 26,000.[^1][^5]
- **PhonePe is going public — again.** The Walmart-backed giant revived its IPO on November 25 reports: targeting a **February–March 2027 listing at a $12–15 billion (₹1.2–1.5 lakh crore) valuation** via a ₹12,000 crore all-OFS, with updated papers to be filed by December-end and bankers re-engaged (ET first reported the $12–14 billion framing).[^11][^13] The timing is regulatory as much as market: PhonePe wants clarity by December-end on **both the UPI-MDR framework and the 30% market-share cap** for third-party UPI apps before refiling — meaning the MDR decision now has a named counterparty with a deadline. The listing economics also just improved: lending reportedly grew ~100% and operating revenue 35–40% QoQ, with the company expected to flip to net profit after FY26's ₹2,792 crore loss.[^11]
- **UPI MDR: still dateless, now with an election asterisk.** The 0.4% fee on eligible P2M transactions above ₹2,000 (capped ₹300) — originally slated for October 15, deferred past Diwali — could now slip **past the 2027 assembly elections** (UP, Punjab, Uttarakhand, Goa, Manipur), with government, RBI and NPCI yet to agree a date even as groundwork completes; the steering committee's flat ₹5 B2B/M2M framing and >95% of P2M staying outside the net remain the shape of the final deal.[^14][^17] Each delay report has whipped the fintech tier — Paytm rose ~11%, MobiKwik 12% and Pine Labs 21% around the September announcement, and gave much of it back on the November delay reports — so the date notification stays the sector's single largest catalyst, now complicated by PhonePe's IPO clock.[^14]
- **The fintech tier is trading the rebound, not the catalyst.** Thursday's board: CCAVENUE +5.72% (hammer off a fresh 52-week low), POLICYBZR +4.11% (third straight green on IRDAI-revision relief), PAYTM +2.90%, PINELABS +1.56% — with no new MDR news. That reads like the November MDR-scare washout stabilising; MobiKwik's flat close despite its strongest-ever H1 (net profit ₹31.4 crore, revenue +120%) shows the market still won't pay for payments fundamentals without the fee.[^16]
- **Macro plumbing:** record November FPI inflows of ₹1.42 lakh crore have powered the Nifty's best month since 1993 (+12%), but Thursday saw FIIs buy a smaller ₹4,479 crore (Wednesday) and the rupee give back 31 paise to 92.55 after its 8-month high — while Brent's push toward $97 and the US's proposed 17% India tariff are the two exogenous risks into month-end.[^7][^8][^9][^6] US markets reopen only for a half-day Friday — expect thin liquidity and exaggerated afternoon moves.[^1]
- **Calendar:** UPI-MDR date notification (PhonePe wants it by December-end); **RBI policy December 4**; IRDAI's revised distribution draft mid-December; PhonePe's updated IPO papers by December-end; Nifty's 26,000 wall and Bank Nifty's 64,000+ reclaim are the technical markers for the week's close.[^1][^11][^13][^14]
