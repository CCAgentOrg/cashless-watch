# Fintech Brief — November 28, 2026

## Today's Top Stories

### RBI's cleanup day: 25 card circulars become one rulebook — and two more Master Directions besides

RBI issued three consolidated Master Directions on November 27, all effective immediately. The headline act is the [Digital Payments – Card-based Transaction Directions, 2026](https://www.corplawupdates.in/updates/rbi-digital-payments-card-based-transaction-directions-2026) (RBI/DPSS/2026-27/477), which repeals **25 circulars issued between September 2011 and March 2024** and reorganises them into five chapters: card standards, security and authentication, tokenisation, card-data storage, and debit-card MDR ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/rbi-consolidates-25-card-payment-circulars-into-one-framework-what-debit-and-credit-card-users-should-know-11795785543520.html); [The Head and Tale](https://theheadandtale.com/explainer/rbi-consolidates-25-existing-card-payment-rules-into-new-directions)).

For cardholders, nothing changes today by design: the ₹5,000 contactless no-PIN limit, existing debit MDR ceilings, tokenisation consent and deregistration rights, and on/off card controls all carry forward unchanged — approvals issued under the old circulars remain valid ([Elets BFSI](https://bfsi.eletsonline.com/rbi-card-payment-directions)). The value is navigability: a merchant checking what MDR they can be charged and a cardholder checking whether they can tap without a PIN now read the same document.

The other two directions landed the same day. The [ATM and PoS Cash Withdrawal Directions](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-atm-pos-cash-withdrawal-directions-2026.html) (RBI/DPSS/2026-27/478) consolidate cash-withdrawal rules and restate the entitlement of five free monthly transactions — financial and non-financial — for savings account holders. The [White Label ATM Operators Directions](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-white-label-atm-operators-directions-2026.html) (RBI/DPSS/2026-27/479) keep the ₹100 crore net-worth floor and formalise interoperable card-less withdrawals at white-label ATMs, with NPCI advised to enable UPI-based authorisation across banks and ATM networks ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-issues-white-label-atm-operators-directions-2026.html)).

Consumer takeaway: consolidation is boring in the best way — no new rights, no new burdens. The real test is whether a single rulebook makes enforcement clearer the next time a card-data or MDR dispute lands on a consumer's desk.

### CRR floor eased back to 90% as system liquidity stays tight

Separately, RBI [cut the minimum daily CRR maintenance](https://rbi.org.in/scripts/bs_circularindexdisplay.aspx/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx?Id=13759) from 99% back to 90% of the requirement, effective the fortnight beginning December 1 (RBI/2026-27/310, November 27) — reversing the tightening of October 9 ([India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/business/story/rbi-eases-daily-crr-requirement-to-90-from-99-what-does-it-mean-3048503-2026-11-27)). The fortnightly average requirement is unchanged; banks get back intraday room to lend instead of parking buffers at the central bank. The move is a response to a banking system that has shifted from surplus into deficit liquidity, with overnight rates firming. Nothing here touches deposits directly — but tight liquidity is exactly when lenders get picky and expensive, so a modest easing of that pressure is welcome for borrowers.

### PhonePe revives its IPO at $12–15 billion — while its UPI duopoly share slips

PhonePe has restarted its IPO process after pausing it in March, targeting a February–March 2027 listing at a **$12–15 billion valuation**, raising **$1.2–1.5 billion entirely via offer-for-sale** (about ₹12,000–14,000 crore). Revised papers go to SEBI within 30–45 days; Walmart trims roughly 12%, while Tiger Global and Microsoft exit fully ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/walmart-backed-phonepe-restarts-ipo-process-eyes-up-15-billion-valuation-market-2026-11-25); [Economic Times](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/us-stocks/news/phonepe-resumes-ipo-process-targets-listing-in-feb-mar-at-usd-12-15-bn-valuation/articleshow/136160163.cms); [HuBL](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/phonepe-revives-ipo-plans-targets-12-15-billion-valuation-in-early-2027/article71742261.ece/amp)). The company has returned to ~40% revenue growth excluding MDR and incentives, its lending business has doubled, and it processed 11.4 billion UPI transactions in October across 650 million-plus registered users.

Two regulatory clouds hang over the debut. NPCI's 30% market-share cap for third-party UPI apps — PhonePe held 46.85% of customer-initiated transaction volume in its draft prospectus disclosures — has a compliance deadline of December 31, 2026, and the UPI MDR framework is still settling ([Trade Brains](https://tradebrains.in/indian-markets/phonepe-ipo-why-could-it-be-forced-to-give-up-its-upi-market-share-ahead-of-listing-12843615)). Meanwhile the duopoly is leaking share: PhonePe and Google Pay's combined UPI app share fell to **77.4% in October** ([MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/11/223-phonepe-google-pay-upi-share-october-2026)).

Consumer takeaway: at $15 billion, PhonePe would be India's largest fintech debut since Paytm's 2021 float — a comparison that should make every retail investor read the risk factors twice. A forced moderation of UPI market share before listing is the variable nobody can price yet.

### Matters.AI raises ₹115 crore as fintech money chases AI-agent security

Cybersecurity startup Matters.AI raised **₹115 crore (about $12 million) in pre-Series A** funding led by Tanglin Venture Partners, with Kalaari Capital, Endiya Partners and IIFL Fintech Fund participating — its bet is protecting sensitive data from misuse by AI agents ([TICE](https://www.tice.news/tice-dispatch/startup-news-november-27-2026-dpiit-flipkart-healthkart-12845984); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/11/weekly-funding-roundup-nov-21-27-vc-inflow-dips-on-modest-deal-activity)). It rounds out a week in which India's payments industry positioned hard for agentic commerce: Cashfree launched a wallet that lets AI agents pay from a user-funded balance, and NPCI's Unified Agent Protocol for UPI remains in development — still without public liability rules for when an agent gets it wrong.
